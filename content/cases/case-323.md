## Corrompu (France) — October 31, 1954

Near Long, P. Petit and his em- ployee, Mr. Tillier, with a shop owner, Mr. Pecquct, saw an oval object on the ground. When it took off, the lights of a tractor went out. Il measured about 4.5 m, emitted a bright light similar to a welder's torch. It came back, turned, and flew to the south- west. It made the same noise as a swarm of bees. (Personal)

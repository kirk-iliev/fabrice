## Mogadore (Ohio) — May 9, 1964 (1100)

Three children, John Owens, Cheryl Glunt, and Bernie Montello, saw a silvery, dome-shaped object arrive from the south and land in a field 150 m away. Estimated diameter, 3 m. (FS June, 65)

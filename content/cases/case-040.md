## Case 40 — Unknown date

Aug., 1914 Georgian Bay (Canada). William }. Kiehl and seven other persons saw a spherical craft on the surface of the water. On its deck were two small men wearing green-purple clothes. They seemed to be busy with a hose, plunging it into the water. On the opposite side were three men dressed in light brown, wearing square masks down to their shoulders. Seeing the witnesses, they reentered the craft except for one dwarf, wearing shoes with a curved, pointed tip, who remained out- side while the craft rose 3 m above the water and shot upward, leaving a short trail. (199)

## Duas Pontes (Brazil) — August 19, 1962

Raimundo, the son of Rivalino night da Silva (see previous case), was awakened by the sound of steps and saw "a weird shadow" in the room. It was small and not human in shape. Voices were heard saying, "This one looks like Rivalino," and later that they would kill him. The family stayed on the alert all night. (APRO Sept., 62)

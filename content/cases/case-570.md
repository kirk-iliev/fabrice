## Yvrac-Maille (France) — May 15, 1963

Mr. and Mrs. D., of Carig- nan, saw an object on the ground to the left of the road as they were driving between Bergerac and Bor- deaux, beyond the Yvrac intersection, 2.5 km from Maille. They stopped to observe it, and the object then followed them for part of their trip. (LDLN 71)

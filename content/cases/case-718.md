## Springhill (Nova Scotia) — November 29, 1965

Kevin Davis, 12, and Gary evening Jardine, 10, saw an object with a blinking red dome fly over the Cooper Creek area and come to ground level. They observed portholes and a long bar with fingerhke devices emerging from an opening. Snow was blown away and bushes were flattened. (NICAP Jan, 66)

## Hamburg (New York) — June 23, 1966 (2130)

A 61-year-old civilian woman was reading when an intense red light illuminated the ground near her house. She went outside and saw a lighted object, 20 m away, I m in diameter, which backed up and flew away "like a bullet." Three other persons saw it from the next house. (Atic)

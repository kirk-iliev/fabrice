## Malvern (Great Britain) — October 25, 1967 (2045)

Clive Robinson and Rich- ard Corben, 14, and a third boy, reported the landing of a dome-shaped object with a smaller hump on top, 200 m away. It took off diagonally, leaving some traces. (FSR 68)

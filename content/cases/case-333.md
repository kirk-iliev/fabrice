## La Tessoualle (France) — November 8, 1954

Andre Chaillou felt pricklings dusk in his hands, "in spite of his gloves," when a blue disk came close to his motorcycle and his electrical system failed. He was unable to move or articulate a word or several minutes. As soon as the blue light was urned off. he started again and went near the light when it reappeared 200 m away. He saw the object, acone 5.5 m in size, rise vertically with a soft whistling, hen fly horizontally to the north. (98)

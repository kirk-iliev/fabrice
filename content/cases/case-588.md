## Saltwood (Great Britain) — November 16, 1963

Four teenagers, among evening them painter John Flaxton, while walking on a country road, saw a moving star turn into a reddish glow coming toward them. then disappearing behind some trees. Shortly thereafter, a bright, golden light was seen 80 m away, floating 3 m above ground, and a dark figure the size of a man, with wings like a bat, came toward them. They fled in terror. (FSR 64, 2; Magonia)

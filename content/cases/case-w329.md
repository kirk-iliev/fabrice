## Quedlinburg, Germany — 7 February 1802

### Dark celestial body

An unknown dark body is again observed crossing the Sun, "having rapid motion of its own" by amateur astronomer Fritsch. The tiny spot crossed the Sun in a northwesterly direction and accelerated.

**Source:** E. Ledger, "Observations or supposed observations of the transits of intramercurial planets or other bodies across the Sun's disk," The Observatory 3 (1879-80): 136. 377.
**Case:** W329

## Sandusky Road (Ohio) — June 8, 1966 (0645)

A civilian man, 43, driving west between Kansas and Toledo, saw an object ap- pear out of nowhere. It was cigar-shaped, bright metallic, and flew low to the northeast. Minimum distance: 30 m. The object was completely silent, and the size of an airliner. (Atic)

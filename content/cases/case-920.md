## Lins (Brazil) — October 9, 1968 (0620)

Doribio Pereira, 41, municipal em- ployee, suddenly observed a golden, cigar-shaped object, and a figure armed with a flashing weapon that immobilized him. Three beings were standing on a platform under the craft, and another figure appeared o be using a keyboard inside the transparent top part. The object took off rapidly, leaving the witness in a state of shock. (FSR 69, 1)

## Sandy Creek (Australia) — June 28, 1963 (2130)

A fiery red object, 8 m wide, 4m high, with a concave top and flat bottom, was seen on the road by a Willaston resident who stopped his car 4 m away. The object rose, tipped to one side, and flew away at fantastic speed. (141; FSR 64, 1)

## Athens, Greece: Solar intruder — 18 February 1850

### 

An unknown body was seen passing in front of the Sun. It was observed and reported by astronomer Schmidt.

**Source:** R. C. Carrington, "In the 10th number of Professor Wolf's Mittheilungen iiber die Sonnenflecken..." Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society 20 (January 1860): 100-1.

## Lisbon, Portugal — 17 September 1680

### Landing of a hairy occupant

A large black cloud-like mass landed in a field, releasing a huge hair-covered being that appeared - and disappeared.

**Source:** A photocopy of the pamphlet, written by Julio Alberto de la Hinojosa, was reproduced in its entirety in Fenomenos Celestes en el Pasado: Siglos VIII al XIX, published by the Centra de Estudios Interplanetarios, Barcelona, 1995.

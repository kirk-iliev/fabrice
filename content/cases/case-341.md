## Forli (Italy) — November 14, 1954

A strange beam of red light, apparently night from some flying source, was reported sweeping the countryside. As it illuminated two tractors, one of them stalled, but the other, a diesel, continued. The beam was seen for about one hour by a large number of persons. (M 211)

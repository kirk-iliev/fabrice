## Grand Forks (North Dakota) — August 9, 1965 (2330)

Three girls driving a car observed what they first thought was the rising moon. It was a circular, pale-yellow object. with a silhouette moving inside the glow, There were five other witnesses, one of whom stated that as he ap- proached the object, it appeared to "blow up" with a bang. (FSR66, 2)

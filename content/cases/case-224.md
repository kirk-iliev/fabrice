## Mahallat (Persia) — October 10, 1954

Approximate date. Many farmers observed the landing of a "marvelous luminous ob- ject,” hemispherical in shape, emitting multicolored beams. The central part appeared to be metallic. The object shot straight up suddenly and vanished while the crowd watched. (Creighton; 52)

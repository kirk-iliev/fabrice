## Boardman (Ohio) — July 18, 1967 (0130)

A minister was awakened by a strange sound and had the to go down- stairs and look outside. Between his house and the next one, he saw a silhouette wearing a luminous suit. He thought someone was playing a prank, but the apparition was well-defined and looked real. It turned into a shapeless glow and vanished. (Keel; Magonia)

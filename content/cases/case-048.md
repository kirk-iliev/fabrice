## Summer, 1933 Chrysville (Pennsylvania) — 1933

A man observed a faint Morning violet light in a field between this town and Morres- town. Walking to it, he found an ovoid object 3 m in diameter and 2 m thick with a circular opening similar to a vault door. Pushing it, he found the room full of violet light and observed many instruments, no oc- cupant. Smell of ammonia. (APRO July, 64)

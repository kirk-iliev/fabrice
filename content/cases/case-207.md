## Saint-Jean-d'Asse (France) — October 7, 1954 (0620)

On Route N138, a truck- driver, Mr. Tremblay, saw an intense, blue light com- ing toward him. The object producing it was cigar- shaped, red and blue. Engine and headlights died. (41: M 143)

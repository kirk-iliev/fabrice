## Chumbicha (Argentina) — December 11, 1962

G. L. Colodrero and the di- dawn rector of the Cordoba Historical Museum were driv- ing from Catamarca to Cordoba when, 7 km away from Chumbicha, they saw seven objects on the ground of a mountain slope. They were bright and spherical, rose straight up, and flew away with a very bright trail. (CODOVNI 1962)

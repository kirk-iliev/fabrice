## Italy: Great ship in the air — 19 February 1465

### 

From the Notabilia Temporum of Angelo de Tummulillis: "There appeared many signs in the air in the same month, always in the morning, at daybreak. At the first hour of the 19th of this month a kind of great flaming ship appeared in the air towards the north and it appeared again on the 20 and 21 , not at the same time but later." th st

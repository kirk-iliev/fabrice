## Criacao do Cabrito (Azores) — February 1, 1968

Serafim Vieira Se- bastiao, a watchman at the Azores Air Station, saw "an oval object with a metallic gleam, topped by a glass tower with a small balustrade on which two beings were standing." There were two other figures inside the tower. When he shone a light toward it, a cloud of dust surrounded the witness, the craft van- ished, and he fainted. Investigation by American mili- tary authorities. (FSR 68, 5)

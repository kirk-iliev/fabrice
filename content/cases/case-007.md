## Between Melbourne and Sydney at sea (Australia) — June 11, 1881 (0400)

The two sons of the Prince of Wales, one of (hem the future king of England, were cruising aboard "La Bacchante” when an object resembling a fully lighted ship was seen ("a phantom vessel all aglow"). (Fort 637, Anatomy 12)

## Baskatong Lake (Canada) — November 6, 1957 (2100)

180 km north of Ottawa, Jacques Jacobson and three of his friends saw a bright, yellowish-white sphere hovering over a hilltop about 4km away. From top and bottom issued light cones that illuminated the countryside and the clouds. Radio reception was blocked throughout the obser- vation, except for a very powerful signal at one wave- length, modulated, but not in Morse code. The object rose slowly toward the south. (M 249)

## Le Rouret (France) — June 18, 1966 (0400)

A truck driver, Mr. Dugclay, saw a disk-shaped object over Le Rouret. It remained motionless for five min, then disappeared. Its lights were alternately red and bluish-green. (LDLN 84; 163)

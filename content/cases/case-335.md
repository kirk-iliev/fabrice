## Porto Alegre (Brazil) — November 10, 1954

Porto Alegre (Brazil), An agronomist and his family in their car saw a landed disk from which two men, normal in height, with long hair and coverall-like clothes, emerged and came toward the car with arms raised. As the frightened witnesses sped away, they saw the men reenter the disk, which rose and flew off at very great speed. (Lor. I 42)

## Riachuclo (Chile) — February 24, 1967

Approximate date. An engineer from Osorno, well-known locally, saw an object touch down and a strange being, 1.50 m tall, emerge, wear- ing a transparent suit.He had a very white face and white hands, a pronounced jawbone, and no apparent nose. Seeing the witness, he got back inside the ma- chine, which took off at great speed. (Lor. Ill 59; Vuillequez)

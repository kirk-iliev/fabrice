## Crystal Lake (Montana) — March 9, 1963

Amos Biggs observed a sil- ver, saucer-shaped craft, with an oval dome, which landed on the frozen lake for 10 min. A "door" was opened and then shut, and the craft took off with a buzzing sound. (FS July, 64)

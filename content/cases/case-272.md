## Cier-de-Riviere (France) — October 16, 1954

Guy Puyfourcat, 22, who was coming back from the fields, leading a mare by he bridle, was surprised when the animal became restless. Rising from the side of the road, a gray object about 1.5 m in diameter flew over them. The mare rose about 3 m in the air, and the witness had to re- ease the bridle. Then the animal fell like a mass and ‘or 10 min was unable to move. At last, it rose and attempted to walk, but it was still trembling and stumbling with fear. The object had long since flown away at high speed. The witness himself had felt absolutely nothing. (Personal)

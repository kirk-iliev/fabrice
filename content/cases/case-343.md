## Calcerosa (Italy) — November 25, 1954 (1700)

Two 12-year-old boys, G. Marziano and P. Santucci, suddenly saw three figures who, as soon as they were discovered, entered a small spherical craft concealed 10 m away behind some bushes. The beings were small, about 35 cm tall, had very large heads and lead-gray skin. The craft had two sharp- pointed propellers in front, which started spinning. The craft took off suddenly with a hissing sound. (90)

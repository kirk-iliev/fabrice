## Sonderborg (Denmark) — June 19, 1951 (1200)

Joseph Matiszewski, a me- chanic, heard a whistling sound and saw an objec land in a meadow. Approaching within 50 m, he found himself paralyzed and observed that birds had stopped singing and cows seemed to be similarly un- able to move. From the object emerged four hand- some men who had brown skin and wore black shiny suits and translucent helmets. Eight objects also emerged from the craft and hovered above it. Other men inside the craft and on its deck appeared to be making repairs, then the objects flew to about 100 m altitude and climbed rapidly out of sight. Only then did the paralysis subside. (Nachrichten May, 59)

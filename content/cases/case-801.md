## Newton (Illinois) — October 14, 1966 (1845)

A adolescent first saw a bright light, then a plate-shaped object hovering near the house. It took off at high speed. causing static on the phone as the boy was calling his mother to describe it. When she came home, she found her son in a state of shock and the dog hiding in a corner. (Personal)

## Vilhelmina (Swedish Laponia) — March 4, 1967 (2400)

The Sodcstrom fam- ily observed two plate-shaped objects, one 30 m in diameter, the other smaller and gray in color. They hovered at 20 m altitude for 15 min. The object left with a hushed whistling sound, illuminating the forest with intense light. (171)

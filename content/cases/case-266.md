## Nimes-Courbessac Airfield (France) — October 15, 1954 (1950)

A yellow, cigar- shaped object with brilliant portholes, 30 m long, 6 m diameter, was seen on the ground. Figures with hel- mets covering the head could be seen inside. A sort of haze was observed at both ends of the craft. (Quincy)

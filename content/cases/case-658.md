## Chanaral (Chile) — July 19, 1965

Carlos Videla Zamudio saw a night "strange machine" land on an isolated beach. It was shaped like a mushroom or a top, was lighted from inside, rested on the ground silently, flew up to 30 in, and disappeared at fantastic speed. This was the third landing on a beach reported in July. (155; LDLN 78; APRO Jan., 66)

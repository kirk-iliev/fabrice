J. Simonton heard a whin- ing sound and saw an object, 10 m in diameter, 4 m high, with exhaust pipes around the periphery, land near his house. A door was opened and a man ap- peared. About 1.50 m tall, he wore a black. turtle-nec! pullover with a white band at the belt, and blac! rousers with a vertical white band along the side. Two figures were visible inside the object.

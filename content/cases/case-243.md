## Montlucon (France) — October 12, 1954

A railroad employee, Mr. evening Laugerc, saw a torpedo-shaped, metallic craft on the ground near a gas-oil tank. An individual covered with hair was standing nearby, emitting sounds that were not understood. The witness went to get his friends, but the thing disappeared in the meantime. (63; M 167)

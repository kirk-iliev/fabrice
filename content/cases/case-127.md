## Santa Maria (Brazil) — 1954

Rubem Hellwig again saw a early strange machine the next day. He observed a tall, fair- complexioned man and two women, who had light brown skin, long black hair, dark, slanted eyes. All three wore one-piece garments of a fabric resembling suede, with zippers. They told Hellwig that they were scientists, spoke of the natural riches of Brazil, and expressed surprise that he did not run away. (1; Humanoids 33)

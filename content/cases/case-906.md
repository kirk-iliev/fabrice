## May, 1968 Chascomus (Brazil) — Unknown date (2400)

Gerardo Vidal and his wife were driving along Route 2 when they were caught in "a dense fog" and lost consciousness for 48 hours. When they came to, it was daytime, and the car, whose paint was badly scorched, was parked in an un- known road. They spoke to local people and found that they were in Mexico. (FSR 68, 5; Magonia)

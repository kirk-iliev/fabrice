## Caledon East (Canada) — October 31, 1958 (1550)

A civilian reported an elliptical, aluminum-colored object at 2 km altitude, coming down to 4 m, flying up and down by sudden jumps, stopping at ground level less than 200 m away for five min. A red light appeared at one end of the object, which gradually took a fiery color, then ex- ploded. The witness ran away. (Atic)

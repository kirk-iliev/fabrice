## duly 20,1950 Porto Novo (Brazil) — 1950

Mr. Campello and_ several others in a car saw two huge, silvery objects by the side of the road, one on a hillock, the other on flat ground, about 50 m away from each other. They came within 600 m of the objects, which went away at "an incredible speed." causing a rush of air that rocked the car. (SBEDV 30)

## Quedlinburg, Germany — 20 March 1800

### Fast-moving object

A preacher and amateur astronomer named Fritsch reported an unknown object rapidly crossing the disk of the sun.

**Source:** E. Ledger, "Observations or supposed observations of the transits of intramercurial planets or other bodies across the Sun's disk," The Observatory 3 (1879-80): 136.
**Case:** W327

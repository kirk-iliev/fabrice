## Vron (France) — October 3, 1954 (1845)

Between Rue and Quend on Road D27, Rene Coudette and B. Devoisin were riding bicycles with a third witness when they saw an orange object, shaped like a honeycomb, on the road ahead of them, A strange "man" wearing a diving suit was standing close to it. When they got within 70 m of it, the object took off very fast. (32; M 118)

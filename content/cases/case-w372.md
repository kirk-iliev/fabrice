## Szeged, Hungary — 1 July 1836

### Light globes and a lady in white

418. Globular lights, poltergeist effects and apparitions of an entity resembling "a lady in white" and a dwarf-sized Franciscan monk. The case mainly concerns a so-called haunted house and objects being thrown around by unseen hands. Such manifestations are not uncommon in ufological literature and have been related by some researchers to the abduction phenomenon.

**Source:** The story comes from a letter by a Dr. V. Stantsky which was sent to Justinius Kerner and published in the latter's periodical: Magikon, Archive fiir beobachtungen aus dem Gebiete der Geisterkunde und des magnetischen und magischen Lebens, vol. 3 (Stuttgart, 1846), 223-237; William Howitt, "Throwing of Stones and Other Substances by Spirits," The Spiritual Magazine, vol. VI, 3 (Feb. 1865): 55-56.

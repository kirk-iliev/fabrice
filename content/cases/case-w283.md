## Bucharest, Romania: Red intruder — 6 December 1737

### In the afternoon, an object only described as a "Symbolic form," blood-red in color,

appeared from the west. After remaining in the sky for two hours it split into two parts that shortly joined again and went back towards the west.

**Source:** Ion Hobana and Julien Weverbergh, Les Ovni en URSS et dans les Pays de I'Est (Paris: Robert Laffont, 1972), 287-288, citing Biblioteca Academiei Romane, BAR ms. rom. 2342, fol. 3-4.

## Oye-Plage (France) — October 27, 1954 (2315)

On Route N40, the mayor's secretary saw for 15 min a bright, cigar-shaped object. flying very low and following the turns of the road at 20 m altitude. Suddenly it turned at a right angle from the road and flew away. (Personal)

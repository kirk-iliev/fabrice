## Portglenone (Ireland) — December 28, 1958

A black flying object, 2 m afternoon wide, cut a tree in two, 3 m above ground, and did not stop. The tree in question is 70 cm wide and 13 m tall. This event was observed by a farmer named Bennett. (FSR59, 2)

## Benet (France) — October 3, 1954 (2300)

Near La Rochelle. Mr. and Mrs. Guillemoteau saw an object, 2.5 m high, 5 m diam- eter, hover for several minutes 1 m above ground, then rise vertically. Oily marks were found at the spot. (34, M 131}

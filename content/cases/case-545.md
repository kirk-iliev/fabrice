## Mount Manfre (Italy) — September 5, 1962

Second observation by Mr. night Siragusa (Case 531), who saw two figures over 2.10 m all. The light from their belts prevented him from seeing them in detail. A large, spinning object. 25 m wide, top-shaped, hovered nearby. "From the under part, a metallic cylinder over 3 m long reached down almost to touch the road, with a small door, a sort of lift." (FSR 62, 6)

## Bradford (England) — August 19, 1955 (2330)

About 700 m away from Round- hill street, Mr. Wood, a warehouseman, saw a bright, bullet-shaped, silvery object behind a hillock. It measured about 4 m in height, 15 m in diameter, had a surface similar to chromium and made a high- pitched buzzing sound. (Constance 222)

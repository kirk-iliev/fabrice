## Mesples (France) — October 29, 1954 (0745)

A 14-year-old child slight asylum in the farmhouse owned by Mrs. Gentil, crying that he had been chased by a saucer. A disk three times as large as the sun, red and purple, spinning rapidly, was seen descending swiftly toward the ground, and then it vanished. Investigation by local police. (Personal)

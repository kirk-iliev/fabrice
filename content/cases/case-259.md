## Saint-Romain (France) — October 14, 1954

Mr. B. saw a circular craft nightfall shaped like an upside-down plate. At the same time. the engine on his motorcycle stalled. In the same area an engineer saw a luminous object coming down rapidly. (Anatomy 130)

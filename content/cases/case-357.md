## Valencia (Venezuela) — December 20, 1954 (0000)

Valencia (Venezuela) An employee of the Barbula sanatorium saw an object on the ground but did not report it. Three hours later, another employee saw the craft. It was luminous and took off soon after- ward. (Lor. I 52)

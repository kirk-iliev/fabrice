## Dorf-Gull (Germany) — December 9, 1954 (1930)

Ernst Jung and his wife saw an intense red, luminous object descending toward them at great speed. It hovered 5 m above ground when they stopped their motorcycle. It was cigar- shaped, 5 m long. 2 m wide. They saw it turn off its lights and fly toward the forest. Other people in a bus saw the same thing. (Nachrichten Oct., 56)

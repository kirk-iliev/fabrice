## Statford Row, near London, England — August 1660

### A Great ship in the air

The likeness of a "great ship" was seen in the air. It decreased in size and eventually disappeared. The worthy chronicler does not fail to inform us that "this is testified by an able Minister living not far from the place, who received the information from the spectators themselves".

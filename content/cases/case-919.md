## Mendoza (Argentina) — August 31, 1968

Three witnesses, among them early two Casino employees, stated that they observed five dwarfs, with oversized heads, who emerged from a anded craft and traced undecipherable signs on the ground, (he Figaro Sept. 2, 1968)

## Salina (Venezuela) — August 8, 1967

Evangelic Pastor Estanislao evening Lugo Contreras was at the shore when he saw a disk- shaped object with a very bright orange glow emitting a buzzing sound, rise out of the sea, hover a few sec- onds then rise obliquely. (Lor. [11 55)

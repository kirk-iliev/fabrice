## Grand-Couronne (France) — September 30, 1954 (2200)

Jean Andrieux, ferry operator, saw a large white sphere, with a smaller green sphere below, hang motionless above the Seine for 20 min. Two witnesses. It eventually flew away to the southwest. (27)

## Chaleix (France) — October 4, 1954

A farmer, Mr. Garrcau, saw an object the size of a carriage land in his field. Two men of normal height emerged through a sliding door. They wore khaki overalls and were of European type. They shook hands with the witness and said some- thing like: "Paris? Nord?" (According to another version, they spoke indistinct words.) They gave a pat on the back to Mr. Garreau's dog and took off at an amazing speed. (37; Magonia)

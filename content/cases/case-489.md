## The Willows (South Africa) — June 21, 1959 (2000)

A civilian woman ob- served a saucer-shaped object hovering 5 m above ground. It was bathed in an orange glow, similar to that of a dying fire. It flew away horizontally and was lost to sight behind a hill. (FSR 59, 6)

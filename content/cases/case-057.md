## Spokane (Washington) — June 21, 1947 (1150)

Eight disk-shaped objects, the size of a house, were seen flying at 1000 km/h. A civilian woman stated that the objects fell with a dead- leaf motion and landed before ten witnesses on the shore of the Saint Joe River, in Idaho. (Atic)

## Boyup Brook (Australia) — October 30, 1967 (2100)

A businessman was driving toward Boyup Brook when his car failed completely and stopped, although he did not recall feeling de- celeration. He found a strong light beam aimed at him from a mushroom-shaped craft, 10 m in diam- eter. He had no recollection of starting the car again after the departure of the object. (FSR 68, 4)

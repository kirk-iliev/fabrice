## Pinhal (Brazil) — October 30, 1965

Witnesses in a Volkswagen en- night countered a strong light on the road. A truck and another Volkswagen arrived and went near the object, which took off suddenly and flew over them. It was also observed by dozens of witnesses at the Pinhal Junction, (APRO Sept., 66)

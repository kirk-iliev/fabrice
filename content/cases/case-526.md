## Lafayette (Indiana) — December 21, 1961 (2145)

Jerry Hislope, 20, was driving o Kentland when he saw a glowing white object, 3 m in diameter, 1 m thick, dive at him, flying 3 m above the ground. He stopped to observe it, but the object flew away. (APRO Mar, 62)

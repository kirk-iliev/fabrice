## Case 376 — September 17, 1955

Bush Pine {New York). Mr. and Mrs. Bordes, of New night York City, were fishing when they heard a loud splash and a "gurgling sound,” saw a pink, iridescent, mush- room-shaped object rise about 70 cm above the water, and sink into the reservoir. Later, they saw the object again, about 5 m long and surrounded with turbu- lence. It reversed direction several times without turn- ing around and finally flew away very fast (Constance 226, FSR 55, 5)

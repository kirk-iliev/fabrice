## Stranraer (Scotland) — August 14, 1965 (0200)

Patrick Nash and his family were awaiting a ferry boat when a brilliant, orange-red object shaped like a large soup plate appeared 50 m away, rose from the ground, flew low over the car, landed 20 m away on the other side, vanishing sud- denly. (FSR65, 6)

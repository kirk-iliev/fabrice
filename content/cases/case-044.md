## Case 44 — 1922 (0500)

Feb.22,1922 Hubbell (Nebraska). William C. Lamb was follow- ing strange tracks when he heard a high-pitched sound and saw a circular object intercepting starlight. It became brilliantly lighted and landed in a hollow. Soon afterward, a creature over 2.4 m tall was seen flying from the direction where the object had landed. It left tracks in the snow, which Lamb followed with- out results. (Anatomy 22)

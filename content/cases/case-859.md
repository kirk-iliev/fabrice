## Case 859 — July 24, 1967 (2200)

July 24,1967 Mareuil (France), Between Mareuil and Ste-Her- mine, Daniel Bonifait and his family saw a red disk. he size of a building, fall 300 m away behind a hill. Dark forms were observed on the disk’s surface, and the forest seemed to be ablaze as the witnesses drove away in fear. (GEPA Dec, 68)

## Guanare (Venezuela) — 1954

Guanare (Venezuela), Early in December, the di- rector of Barquisimeto College was chased by a lumi- nous disk as he was driving near Guanare. He fired at the object with his revolver, without effect. He stopped another car in which a lawyer and two police- men were traveling. All four saw the machine fly away. (Lor. I 43)

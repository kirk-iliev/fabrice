## Don court-Village (France) — October 11, 1954 (0130)

Don court-Village (France), Farmers awakened by a whistling sound saw a flat object land near the woods. It soon rose very fast, vertically, and flew away. The ground was found calcined. Samples were taken by police officials for analysis. (56)

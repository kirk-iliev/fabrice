## Ugines (France) — November 25, 1957 (1430)

Two engineers were driving about 200 m from the railroad crossing in Faverges when hey saw a nearly spherical object at ground level, performing zig-zags on the road. They stopped and were amazed as the object simply vanished, leaving no trace. (Personal)

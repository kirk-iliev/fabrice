## Montville (Ohio) — November 6, 1957

Olden Moore, 28, a plasterer, was 2330 driving home when he suddenly saw an object look: ing like a bright meteor split into two pieces, one of which went straight up. Tlie other got larger while its color changed from bright white to blue-green. It hovered 60 m above a field and came to the ground with a soft whirring sound, 150 m away. After observ- ing it for 15 min, Moore then walked to the object, which he found to be shaped like "a covered dish" 15 m in diameter, 5 m high, with a cone on top about 3 m high, surrounded by haze or fog, pulsating slowly. Holes, footprints and radioactivity were found at the site by Civil Defense Director Kenneth Locke. (M 252)

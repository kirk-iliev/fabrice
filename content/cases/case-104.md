## Marignane Airport (France) — October 27, 1952 (0203)

Customs officer Ga- briel Gachignard observed a cigar-shaped object land briefly on the airfield 100 m away, producing a dull sound. The object was dark with four lighted win- dows. It took off with a "swish" and a shower of sparks when the witness ran toward it. (Challenge 6)

## Cherry Creek (New York) — August 5, 1965 (2030)

Several children saw an object shaped like two saucers glued together come to ground level several times on the farm of William Butcher. It was chromelike, measured about 16 m in diameter, took off with green and yellow flames, and illuminated the clouds when it flew into them. Capt. James Dorsey and four technicians from Niagara Falls AFB investigated the case. They found no truce, concluding, however, that the witnesses were not lying. (Personal)

## [ripoli (Libya) — October 23, 1954 (0300)

A farmer saw a flying craft descend o ground level about 50 m away, with a sound like hat of a compressor. It was an egg-shaped machine with six wheels and complex machinery. The top half was transparent, flooded with bright white light. Aboard were six men in yellowish coveralls, having uman faces and masks. When he touched part of he craft, the witness felt a strong electric shock. One of the occupants motioned for him to stay away. For he next 20 min, the witness was able to observe the six men, apparently busy with instruments. Reliable investigations were made. (Personal; Magonia)

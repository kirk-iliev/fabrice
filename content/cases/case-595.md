## Lordsbury (New Mexico) — April 22, 1964 (2100)

Marie Morrow and two other persons were driving west, about 20 km east of Lordsbury when the entire area was illuminated by a bluish light "as bright as day," and a round object flew about 3 m above the car, making a whining sound, then went north. (APRO May, 64)

## Le Jou (France) — September 23, 1954 (2100)

The Patient family witnessed the landing of a bright object giving off a magnesiumlike light, a red glow, and a narrow beam of light. It took off again a moment later and followed their car until they reached Fontland. Policemen in Plombicrcs and several independent witnesses observed the object as it flew erraticaily over the area for 60 min. (13; M 69)

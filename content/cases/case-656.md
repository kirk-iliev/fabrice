## Villas Rosas (Argentina) — July 19, 1965 (1930)

July 19.1965 Villas Rosas (Argentina). Maria Andres, teacher, Mr. Gomez, Mrs. Goicoecha, and others saw a small object leave a larger one, land, and burn a spot on the ground before going back to the main object. It emitted a blinding light. (Asi July 30, 1965)

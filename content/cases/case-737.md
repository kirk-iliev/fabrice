## Attigneville (France) — March 26, 1966 (1330)

Jean Voilquin, 54, saw a strange “wheel" roll 15 m away at a speed of about 30 km/h. It was about 80 cm in diameter, 25 cm thick; it rolled away and vanished. (LDLN; GEPA 8)

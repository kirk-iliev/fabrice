## Bergerac (France) — October 1, 1954 (2200)

Returning home, fireman Jean Dufix, 26, saw an oval light in the sky and though! il was a shooting star. Later he Was alarmed when his yard seemed to be ablaze. Rushing out again, he saw a disk rise from the ground with a whistling sound. It flew off, became luminous again, and took off at fan- tastic speed. A neighbor, Jean Labonne, 61, saw the disk, 3 m in diameter, resting on three legs in the yard. (31)

## La Porte (Indiana) — September 30, 1961 (2200)

Eight km south of La Porte, 16- year-old Dennis Bealor saw a large sphere of orange light rise ahead of him on the road. He was so fright- ened that he lost control of his bike and left the road. (Hartle 158)

## Saint-Hilaire-des-Loges (France) — October 23, 1954

Mrs. Boeuf was coming out of her farmhouse when she saw a lumi- nous disk in the sky and called her family. When everyone saw the object come closer, they locked all doors and spent a sleepless night. They did not ob- serve the object's departure. (81)

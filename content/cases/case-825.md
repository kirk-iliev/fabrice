## Rochester (Minnesota) — March 12, 1967

A boy who had gone out- side to buy a newspaper saw an object resembling an inverted mushroom hovering 10 m above ground. It was not larger than a car, took a 45° orientation, oscillated, and left to the northwest. Three photo- graphs were reportedly taken. (Atic)

## Beaumont (France) — October 5, 1954 (1545)

Ten km from here, several per- sons saw an object coming toward them and getting brighter. When it was about 150 m away, they felt "a strange sensation" and found themselves unable to move. The object left a smell compared to that of nitrobenzine. (34; Challenge 53)

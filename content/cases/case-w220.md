## Goult, Vaucluse, France — 1661

### A luminous figure heals a sick man

267. Antoine de Nantes, a messenger from Goult, who was gravely ill, caught sight of a marvelously beautiful child who hovered above a fiery halo. When this figure vanished, the man found himself fully healed. A chapel was consecrated two years later and "miracles became commonplace after that date."

**Source:** Louis Leroy, Histoire des pelerinages de la Sainte Vierge en France (Paris, 1873), 30.

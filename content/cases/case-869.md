## Joyceville (Canada) — August 23, 1967

Stanley Moxon, of Toronto, early driving to Montreal, was between Joyceville and the Glen Grove Rd. when he encountered a large, disk- shaped craft and two creatures, 120 m tall, dressed in white suits and helmets. They "flew back" into the object at once and it left silently at high speed. (Lor. Ill 32)

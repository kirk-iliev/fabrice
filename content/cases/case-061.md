## Bauru, near Pitanga (Brazil) — July 23, 1947

A group of survey workers ran away as they heard a hissing noise and saw a disk land 50 m away. Jose C. saw two figures through a window. Later three beings in shiny clothes and translucent suits, with oversized bald heads, huge round eyes, no eyelashes or eyebrows, and a metal box on their back, emerged from the craft. They were over 2 m tall. They drew the solar system and pointed to Uranus as if to suggest that was their point of origin. (Round-up 163, FSR 61, 6)

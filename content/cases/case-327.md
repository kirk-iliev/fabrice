## Pontal (Brazil) — November 4, 1954

Jose Alves was fishing in the Pardo night River when he suddenly saw a craft approach with a wobbling motion and landing near him, Shaped like two washbowls placed together, it was about 4,5 m in 242 PASSPORT TO MAGOKIA diameter. Too terrified to move, the witness saw three little men, dressed in white, wearing tight-fitting skull caps, with dark skin, come out of the craft, gather vegetables and water and fly away. (Humanoids 33; Lor. I 44)

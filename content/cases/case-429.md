## Knoxville (Tennessee) — November 6, 1957 (0630)

12-year-old Everett Clark saw a strange object on the ground and four occu- pants, two men and two women, who spoke a lan- guage he thought was similar to German. They went back to the craft in a manner the witness could not understand, for he saw no door. (M 271; 114 Magonia)

## Haynesville (Louisiana) — December 30, 1966

A physics professor driving through a wooded area saw a bright, pulsating glow, 2015 changing from orange to white, in the woods about 17 km away. Coming back the next day, he located traces of burns, and called the Air Force and the Uni- versity of Colorado. (Personal; Magonia)

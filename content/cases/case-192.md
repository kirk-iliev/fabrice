## Oct, 4,1954 Montceau-les-Mines (France) — Unknown date

At Les Chavannes, 830 about 20 people, among them Remy Gaudicourt of Sanvignes, saw a circular, luminous object rise from the vicinity of the railroad tracks. It took off vertically. (M._ 138)

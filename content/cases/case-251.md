## Shamsabad (Iran) — October 14, 1954 (0630)

A man coming out of his house saw a luminous object resembling a bright star. Com- ing near, he observed it was an object 5 m long, near which a "short young man" was standing on a circular piece of metal, laughing at the witness's terrified ex- pression. The witness was 20 m away when the craft took off at unbelievable speed. (52)

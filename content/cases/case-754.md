## Daniels Park (Colorado) — April 7, 1966 (2130)

Six teenagers reported strange lights following their car as they left the site of a picnic in a state of extreme fear. An unknown man, 2.20 m tall, wearing black clothes, had been seen passing in front of the shelter entrance and walking on the roof. (Personal)

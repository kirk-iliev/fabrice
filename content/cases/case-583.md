## Monte Maiz (Argentina) — October 12, 1963 (0330)

E. Douglas, while driv- ing a truck through a violent rainstorm, had to stop when he encountered a large, blinding object, 35 m high, from which three giants, 3 m tall, wearing luminous clothes and strange helmets, emerged. Douglas fired at them, as a red beam burned him. He ran away and found shelter in Monte Maiz. He suf- fered burns similar to ultraviolet exposure. Footprints of large dimension were found at the site. (COD- OVNI 1963; Austr. FSR 8; Magonia)

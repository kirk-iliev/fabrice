## Lawrenceville (Illinois) — June 8, 1964 (2230)

Helen Reed observed a spin- ning object, 5 m above ground, less than 20 m away, coming and going with right-angle turns. It had a dome from which colored light emanated (blue turn- ing to red), and bands of yellow light. The object was ost to sight behind trees in the north. (NICAP July, 64)

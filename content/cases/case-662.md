## Carazinho (Brazil) — July 26, 1965 (2000)

A teenager, Adilon Azevedo, and others who fled, saw two objects at ground level, about 3 m in diameter, 15 m high, with five occupants, wearing luminous helmets, speaking among them- selves in unfamiliar language. One of the beings had a bright object in his hand. The witness experienced headaches for five days. (Humanoids 41)

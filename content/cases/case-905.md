## Syracuse (New York) — March 3, 1968 (0615)

Nick Sgouris, while driving to work, observed a luminous, cigar-shaped object, about 20 m long, showing numerous multi-colored lights. The car almost stopped as the object flew low over head, and the witness was briefly paralyzed. (APRG Bulletin 67)

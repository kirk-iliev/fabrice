## Buzancy, Ardennes (France) — August 5, 1955 (1430)

Messrs. Coisin and Mahieu saw five brown, disk-shaped machines coming down and up again at great speed. One of them flew under the others, then two disks appeared to land 300 m away near the German cemetery, The others flew away toward the south at tremendous speed. (Personal)

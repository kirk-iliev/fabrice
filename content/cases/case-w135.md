## Oettingen, Bavaria — 28 June 1548

### Flying vehicles, red flames

The sky became darker and about twenty flying "vehicles" were seen coming and going above the houses, along with red flames. The witness says he saw the phenomenon on W& occasions: on 28 June and on 26 July 1548.

**Source:** Bruno Weber, Wunderzeichen und winkeldmcker, 1543-1586, Urs Graf Verlag (Zurich: Dietikon, 1972), 93.

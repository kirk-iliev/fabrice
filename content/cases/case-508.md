## Hamburg (Germany) — 1960 (0920)

Mrs. K. St. Bereits was in her garden when she saw an object coming down at great speed. Arriving at ground level, it came toward her, appearing as a disk standing on edge, 7 m in diameter, with a pulsating halo of yellow light. Three beams of light came from a central opening. A sort of haze and "gas stream" was also described. (Nachrichten Jan., 61; FSR 61, 4)

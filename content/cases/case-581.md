## Saskatoon (Canada) — September 19, 1963 (2000)

Four children saw a bright oval object hover in a field and drop something. Approach- ing the site, they were confronted with a 3 m tall man, dressed in "a white monklike suit," who held out his hands and made unintelligible sounds. The children fled in panic, and one girl was admitted to the hos- pital in shock. (Personal)

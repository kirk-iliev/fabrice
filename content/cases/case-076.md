## River Road, near Amarillo (Texas) — April 8, 1950

David, 12, and afternoon Charles, 9; Lightfoot saw a disk land behind a hill and touched it. It was the size of a car tire, about 30 cm high, with a rounded top that rotated and a pivot between the base and the top. It took off very fast. The faces and arms of the boys later became red. (200)

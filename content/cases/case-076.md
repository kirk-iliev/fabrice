## River Road, near Amarillo (Texas) — April 8, 1950

David, 12, and afternoon Charles, 9; Lightfoot saw a disk land behind a hill and touched it. It was the size of a car tire, about 30 cm high, with a rounded top that rotated and a pivot between the base and the top. It took off very fast. The faces and arms of the boys later became red. (200) Apr. 20,1950 Lufkin (Texas). Jack Robertson was driving about night 13 km west of town when he saw a round object about 3 m in diameter hovering about 7 m above him with a dull red glow. It took off with a "swooshing roar" as sparks flew from a slot under it. Minutes later the witness felt a burning sensation on his face. (FS July, 59)

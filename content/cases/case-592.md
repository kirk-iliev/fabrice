## Libreville (Gabon) — December 25, 1963

A fisherman witnessed the land- night ing of a craft, from which a terrifying creature emerged. It was humanoid in shape, spoke sounds he could not understand, left footprints on the sand, and went back to the machine and flew off. (147; 1 .DLN 70) APPENDIX 27

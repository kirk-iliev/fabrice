## Fort Pierce (Florida) — March 23, 1966

Two young men were walking through a wooded area when they saw a bright object that they thought was a balloon covered with fluores- cent paint, but it became so intensely bright that they could not look directly at it. As they walked closer, it exploded, leaving no trace. (Atic)

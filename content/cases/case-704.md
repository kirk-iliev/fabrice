## Fredonia (New York) — September 27, 1965

Addie Jones, 61, saw a silvery 081s object, 10 m in diameter, rise from a wooded area and hover silently before leaving toward the west. Under he object was a boxlike device. Distance to witness: 400 m. No light. (160)

## Saronno (Italy) — April 11, 1962

Between Saronno and Legnano, I. evening Benazzi and three other witnesses saw a peculiar craft fly above them and land on the road. It was intensely luminous. (APRO Sept., 62) 53] Apr. 30,1962 Mount Etna (Italy). Eugenio Siragusa, 43, claimed night o have met two men of normal height, clad in diving suits, with belts emitting intermittent yellow-green- blue light, who delivered to him a peace message in talian. The voice had a metallic tone. Also observed was a very dazzling object, 15 m in diameter. (FSR 63,1)

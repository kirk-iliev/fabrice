## Chalac (Argentina) — February 21, 1965 (2100)

About 50 Toba Indians, includ- ing policemen, saw three littke men with luminous glows emerge from an object that had made several low passes over the village with other flying craft. A photographer took several pictures and noted that the creatures feared the light from his flash camera. The object increased in luminosity as it took off. (CODOVNI; FSR 65, 4)

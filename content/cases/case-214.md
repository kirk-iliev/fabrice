## Montaren (France) — October 9, 1954

Between Montaren and Ser viers, 1 km from "Le Mas Blanc," Dr. Fabre, Mr. Court, and six others saw an orange oval object oscil- lating in mid-air. It was very bright and "fiery," and it came very low. (Personal)

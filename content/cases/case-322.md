## Muro Lucano (Italy) — October 30, 1954 (0930)

Two objects were seen by hunters. One touched the ground 50 m away. It was rhomboidic, and from the bottom a cylinder was hanging. A peculiar sound could be heard. The object shifted its position, and the cylinder hit trees, bounc- ing three times. Then the craft gained altitude and flew away, leaving a bluish trail. (82)

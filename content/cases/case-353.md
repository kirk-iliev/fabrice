## Linha Bela Vista (Brazil) — December 11, 1954 (1700)

Near the site of Case 349, Pedro Morais saw two human beings dressed in "yel- low bags” take a tobacco plant and a chicken, then go away. Their craft "had a bottom like an enormous polished brass kettle," hovered with an oscillating motion, and made a noise like a sewing machine. Its upper part resembled a jeep hood. (Lor. I 46; Human- oids 34)

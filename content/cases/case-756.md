## Pennsylvania — April 11, 1966 (0000)

(Pennsylvania). A civilian man, 43, saw a well-defined object 5 m above ground, 100 m away. t showed a flashing red light, rose, was lost in the fog, then came back at treetop level with a strong whistling sound. Its shape was that of a cigar, 15 m long. It had no wings, tail assembly, or wheels. (Atic)

## Case 569 — May 7, 1963

Kirkhy (Great Britain), Margaret McCutcheon and evening her 13-year-old son reported to the police that, after the house lights blacked out while watching television, they saw an object about 6 m in diameter, with two aerials and a red light flashing, emitting a low buzz, near the house. After one min, it flew away. (FSR 63,4)

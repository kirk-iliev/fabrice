## Pedernales River (Texas) — April 24, 1966 (0330)

Tom M. Lasseter, archi- tect, was camping near the river when he was awak- ened by a fluttering sound and observed a glowing white disk, 7 m in diameter, at treetop level 60 m away. It had a row of lights. (NICAP May, 66)

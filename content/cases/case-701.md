## Guarulhos (Brazil) — September 11, 1965 (0800)

Antonio Pau Ferro saw two ob- jects land. Two dwarfish beings emerged, 70 cm tall, dressed like humans, with "ugly" skin. They appeared to examine some tomato plants, reentered their craft, 6 m in diameter, and flew off. They spoke in a lan- guage which was not understood. (LDLN 84)

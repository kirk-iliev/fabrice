## Crane's Beach (Massachusetts) — September 17, 1966 (0445)

Mr. and Mrs. Ron- ald MacGilvary saw a golden-white, luminous object resting on the beach, with two bright lights flying in and out of the craft. It went away after one hour. (LDLN 88; NICAP Oct., 66)

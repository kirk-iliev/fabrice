## Fermcneuve (Canada) — June 12, 1929 (2300)

Levis Brosscau, 20, was re- turning home when he saw a dark object with a yellow light and his horse became very nervous. Within 6 m of the object four or five dwarfish figures were running back and forth. He heard their pointed, childlike voices, then saw the dark object take off with a ma- chinelike sound and a rush of air. Estimated size of object: 15 m diameter, 5 m high. (GEPA Dec, 68)

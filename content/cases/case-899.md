## Yerecoin (Australia) — November 16, 1967 (1830)

Farm manager Alan Pool, 43, saw an object, 6 m in diameter and 15 m high, with round and square windows, land in a field with a whining noise. It took off and vanished, leaving no trace. (FSR 68, 1)

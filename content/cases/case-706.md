## Aguas Blancas (Argentina) — October 1, 1965

Throe Students, Santos Vallejos, Antonia Aparti, and Adela Sanchez, who were walking to General San Martin School, were attacked by small creatures with greenish skin, The children ran away, and arrived at the school in a state of terror. (CODOVNI)

## Romieres (France) — January 17, 1967

Romieres (France), A woman walking home saw a 184?, beam of light about 1 m in section sweep the ground in her direction. It came from a circular, white object that turned off its main light. She then saw reddish spots before it vanished entirely. Another witness saw the phenomenon from a separate location, 2 km away (LDLN).

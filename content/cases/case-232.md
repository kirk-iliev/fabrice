## Fonfrede, near Chambon Feugerolles (France) — October 11, 1954 (0415)

Bap- tiste Jourdy, who was making the daily collection of milk, was suddenly stopped as the engine and head- lights of his truck died. He got out and saw a light above him. After it crossed the road, the headlights came on again and he was able to restart his truck. (37)

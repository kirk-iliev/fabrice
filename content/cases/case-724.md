## Rexburg (Idaho) — January 29, 1966 (0010)

Two civilians returning from a sporting event observed what they first thought was he moon. After driving for about I km, they saw that it was a flat, well-defined object, which had blocked he road. The object was the size of a truck and cast an intense yellow-orange light on the ground. The witnesses turned around and drove back to Rexburg. (Atic) 725, Feb. 6,1966 Aluche (Spain).

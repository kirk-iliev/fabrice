## Liberty (Missouri) — April 1, 1966 (2400)

Darlene Underwood and her mother saw two starlike objects land in a field. One of them rose rapidly when a train came into view. It went behind a hill, while the second one hovered al tree height with its lights off When the Irain had passed, lxith objects landed again and iwo manlike shadows were seen. Voices shouting in a foreign lan- guage and what sounded like a pig being butchered were heard. Light signals. (IR May, 66)

## Tuly 13,1966 Fontcdcra (Italy) — 1966 (0220)

Camillo Faieta, 35, a lineman, was on duty when a light dazzled him, and he observed that it came from an object on a small island on the Fmissario Canal. Two little men were seen briefly on the ground before the departure of the object. There are four other witnesses. (LDLN 86; FSR 67, 1)

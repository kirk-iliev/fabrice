## Green Lake (Wisconsin) — April 27, 1967 (2200)

Four boys with binoculars observed an object come lower with oscillating mo- tions, illuminating the woods 1 km away. It was cone- shaped, with a blue underside, and a top that changed from red to orange to white. (Atic)

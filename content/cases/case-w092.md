## Forli, Italy: Celestial object — 3 March 1428

### Another case of a celestial object ("asub") in Forli: At 1:30 A.M. a fiery lamp was

observed for about two hours. The city archives also mention "a very high flame in the shape of a tower, and a column of apparent fire rising in the air."

**Source:** Filippo Guarini, I Terremoti a Forli (Croppi, Forli, 1880), 12-13 and 143.
**Case:** W092

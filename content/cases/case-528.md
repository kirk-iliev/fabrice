## Aston Clinton (Great Britain) — February 9, 1962 (0330)

Mr. Wildam, of Luton, noticed that his car was gradually losing speed as he approached a bright, oval thing hovering 10 m above the road. The object was surrounded by a glow, and left at high speed very suddenly. (FSR 62, 2)

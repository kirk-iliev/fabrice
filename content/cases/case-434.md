## Meridian (Mississippi) — November 7, 1957 (0725)

Truck driver Malvin Stevens, 48, was driving to Memphis when, about 23 km northwest of Meridian, he saw an object which ap- peared to have two propellers at either end and a third one on top. Getting out of his truck, he saw three little men about 130 m tall, in gray clothes, with “pasty white faces" that seemed friendly and willing to talk, but he was unable to understand their "I stood there for what seemed like an eternity." They got back into the machine and it took off straight up. There was no ignition inter- ference. An 8-year-old girl from Honse independently reported a round object crossing the sky toward the south. (M 273; 115)

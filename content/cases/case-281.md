## Cape Massulo (Italy) — October 17, 1954

On Capri, an artist, Raffael night Castcllc, saw a disk 5 m in diameter land on the property of Curzio Malaparte. Upon approaching it, he discovered it was not a helicopter and saw four dwarfs wearing coveralls emerge from it. After 30 min the craft made a soft whirring sound and rose vertically, leaving blue sparks. (72)

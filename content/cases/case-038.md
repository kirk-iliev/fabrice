## Invercargill (New Zealand) — 1910 (2300)

Several witnesses— among them the vicar, the mayor, and a policeman —saw a cigar-shaped object hovering at 30 m alti- tude. A man appeared at a lateral door and was heard shouting some words in an unknown language. The opening closed, and the object accelerated and was lost to sight. {Stuart 24)

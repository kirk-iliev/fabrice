## Case 38 — Unknown date (2300)

Jan., 1910 Invercargill (New Zealand). Several witnesses— among them the vicar, the mayor, and a policeman —saw a cigar-shaped object hovering at 30 m alti- tude. A man appeared at a lateral door and was heard shouting some words in an unknown language. The opening closed, and the object accelerated and was lost to sight. {Stuart 24)

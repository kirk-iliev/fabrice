## Dompierre (France) — October 16, 1954

Messrs. Deschamp and Laclotre saw a craft, about 20 m diameter, 2 m high, come 2145 within 4m of the ground for 3 or 4 min. It gave off a yellowish light and a glow extended for 4 m around. (Personal)

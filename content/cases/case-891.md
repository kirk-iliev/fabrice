## Reading (Great Britain) — October 26, 1967 (0430)

A businessman driving north between Hook and Reading was puzzled when the electrical system of his car broke down. He then observed a dull object, 20 m in diameter, 100 m away, at about 15 m altitude. He made the rest of his journey in a trancelike state. {FSR 67. 6)

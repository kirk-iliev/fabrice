## Persian Gulf — May 15, 1879 (2140)

Two very large "wheels" were seen spinning in the air and slowly coming to the surface of the sea. Estimated diameter: 40 m. Distance be- tween the objects: 150 m. Speed: 80 km/h. Duration: 35 min. Witnesses aboard the ship "Vultur." (Round- up 147; Anatomy 12)

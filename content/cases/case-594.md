## Monticello (Wisconsin) — April 3, 1964 (2100)

Four people in a car ob- served a rigid configuration of intense red and white lights, apparently attached to a large object that came to ground level, hovered, and flew off very fast as they were driving about 2 km west of Monticello. (Atic; Challenge 31)

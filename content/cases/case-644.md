## Rivesville (West Virginia) — April 23, 1965 (0800)

A woman observed an object land near her house while she was working in her kitchen. It was shaped like a disk, showed port- holes and a cylinder about 1 m high, with a sliding door from which a small creature, about | m tall, emerged and jumped to the ground. Its face was not clearly visible, but it had pointed ears, a sort of tail, and was linked to the main object by a cable. It wore white clothing, appeared to pick up something from the ground, and reentered the cylinder, which then slid up into the larger white disk. The outside rim started spinning in a counterclockwise motion with a soft whistling sound, and the object rose straight up, out of view. Estimated diameter of the disk: 7 m, (Atic)

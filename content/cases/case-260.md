## Thieulloy-la-Ville (France) — October 14, 1954 (2010)

On the road between Thieulloy-la-Ville and Beauvais, Mr. Covemacker saw an object fly over his car as the headlights died. It went on toward the north, seemingly following a train. (Personal)

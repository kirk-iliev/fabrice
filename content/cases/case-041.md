## Gallipoli (Turkey) — August 21, 1915

During severe fighting in the Dardanelles, a peculiar cloud engulfed a British regi- ment, which was never scen again. This was observed by 22 men of the First Field Company, NZ Army Corps, and stated in an affidavit. (Spaceview 45; LDLN82;Magonia)

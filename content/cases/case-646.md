## Eton Range (Australia) — May 23, 1965 (2100)

Eton Range (Australia), About 70 km from Mackay, Jim Tilse, Eric Judin, and John Burgess saw a cir- cular object, 10 m in diameter, flying erratically, mak- ing a buzzing sound. It had headlights, a tripod landing gear, and supported another disk-shaped de- vice. Police investigation disclosed damaged trees and other landing traces. (FSR 65, 5)

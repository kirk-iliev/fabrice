## Scutari (Turkey) — November 2, 1885

A luminous object circled the har- dawn bor. Altitude: 5-6 m. the whole town. Duration: I-IV2 min, as a bluish-green flame. Then plunged into the sea. Made several circles above the ferryboat pier. (LDLN 48; Anatomy 14)

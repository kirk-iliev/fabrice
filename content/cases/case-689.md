## Callao a (Peru) — August 25, 1965 (1010)

A red object shaped like a plate, emit- ting fire and smoke through two openings in its lower section, shook a school building as it allegedly landed on the roof. Faculty and students at the Santa Leonor College observed the craft, which had two antennae on top, rise spinning and emitting red light beams. It flew off to the northeast. (FSR 67, 6)

## Stavanger (Norway) — January 4, 1958

A woodcutter reported that he saw an object land, and a very tall man with a tanned face, wearing a helmet. stepped from the machine. He went back in after a few minutes; the craft took off "with a noise like a flock of birds." Air Force offi- cers and policemen searched the snow-covered hill for traces. {FSR 58, 2)

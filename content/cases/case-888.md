## Duncan (Oklahoma) — October 21, 1967 (2200)

Four boys were driving east on State Route 7 when they saw three men on the road, who “almost flew off and disappeared." They were about 1.20 m tall and wore shiny blue-green clothing. Their faces looked human, but they had very large ears. The next day a small footprint showing four toes was found in the mud. (Lor. Il 84)

## Duas Pontes (Brazil) — August 17, 1962

Rivalino da Silva, a diamond evening prospector, told his associates that he had seen two strange dwarfs digging a hole near his house. They ran away as he came near them, and moments later an object took off from behind the bushes. It was shaped like a hat and surrounded with a red glow. (APRO Sept., 62)

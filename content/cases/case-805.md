## Gaffney (South Carolina) — November 17, 1966 (0400)

Gaffney (South Carolina), Patrolmen A. G. Huskey and C. Ilutchins saw a dark, spherical machine with a flat rim land near them. Estimated diameter: 7 m. An opening and a short ladder became visible, and a small man, dressed in a shiny gold suit, emerged, came within 6 m of them, and spoke in perfect English before talcing off (FSR 68, 2)

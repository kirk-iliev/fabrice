## Temple (Oklahoma) — March 23, 1966 (0505)

An electronics instructor, who was driving to work at a local Air Force Base, observed an object blocking the road. It was shaped like an air- plane fuselage, 25 m long, with a "bubble" on top resembling the canopy on a B-26, rested on legs, and had very bright aft and forward lights, There was a door and a short stairway to the side of the object, and a man in coveralls appeared to be examining the craft, which bore the identification "TL 4768" (see case 417). When the witness approached, the "pilot” went back inside, a sound resembling that of a high- speed drill was heard, and the object rose. No engine was observed. The witness was familiar with all con- ventional military aircraft. (Atic,; Magonia)

## Huanuco (Peru) — September 1, 1965 (0500)

A worker observed the landing of an oval object on an airfield where he had gone "drawn by a strange feeling.” Four other persons also saw the object, from which a dwarfish creature, 85 cm all, emerged. The entity had a head double the size of a normal human head, and made gestures that were not understood. He reentered the machine, which became illuminated and took off, first vertically, then toward the west. (Personal)

## Conway (South Carolina) — January 29, 1953

Hearing a commotion in Jan, 29,1953 his barn, a farmer observed an object about 7 m long and 4 m wide at treetop level. It was light gray in color and lit up inside. It resembled a half egg. The witness fired his gun at the object. Numerous livestock died "mysteriously" in the area after the sighting. (Personal)

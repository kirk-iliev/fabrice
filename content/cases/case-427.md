## Playa del Rey (California) — November 6, 1957

Richard Kehoe driv- was 0540 ing near the beach when his engine stopped, and so did three other cars. The man got out and observed an egg-shaped object wrapped in “a blue haze" on the beach. Two men, below average height, got out of the craft and asked them questions about their identity, what time it was, etc. They looked normal, wore black leather pants, white belts, light-colored jerseys, and seemed to have yellowish-green skin. They went back inside the craft, which left rapidly, and the cars could then be started. (Humanoids 57)

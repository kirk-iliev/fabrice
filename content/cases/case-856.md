## Belfast (Northern Ireland) — July 17, 1967

E. Browne, walking in a evening wood, saw a disklike, dull, blue-gray object with a rough surface, hovering about 2 m above ground. An opening became visible in the middle section and wo men dressed in silvery clothes emerged, walked in the woods, then reentered the craft, which rose and vanished from sight. (FSR 67, 6)

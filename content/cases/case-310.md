## Angouleme (France) — October 26, 1954 (0600)

Vincent Casamajou and his wife saw a large cauldron-shaped craft, the size of a truck (about 7 m) at 50 m distance, near the road 18 km from this town, going toward Paris. It took off without noise, leaving a white trail. (92)

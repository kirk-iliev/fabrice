## Ciolica Alta (Italy) — October 27, 1954 (1400)

A young man named Fabrizio Bruni heard a strange hissing sound and saw an object in a field. It was stationary, | m above the ground, seemed transparent and emitted a blinding glare. The witness fell on the ground "because of his strong emo- ions” at the sight. The object took off vertically and suddenly, leaving a trail. Very white, glistening hreads were found on the ground.

## Paris, France: Flying intruder — 18 February 1579

A "great and wondrous flying serpent or dragon" appeared, according to a leaflet {"canard") of the time. It was seen by many over Paris from two o'clock in the afternoon until evening.

**Source:** A leaflet at the Library of Amiens entitled Du serpent ou dragon volant, grand et merveilleux, apparu et veu par un chacun sur la ville de Paris, le mercredi XVIII febvrier

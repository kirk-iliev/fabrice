## Dec, 11,1957 Chestnut (Louisiana) — Unknown date

Mary Louise Tobin, a school- eacher, was driving on State Highway 1 when she saw an object that she compared to the rising sun, in the vicinity of a smoking car. The driver, an elderly lady, came out with a child who seemed to have suffered burns. The unknown object went away. The disabled car did not catch on fire. (FS July, 58)

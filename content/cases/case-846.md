## Arbonne (France) — May 24, 1967 (2300)

Remy Deneuville and his family saw a white light to the side of the road, but it was turned off when they drove within 150 m of its loca- tion. Minutes later, they saw a luminous spherical object fly away for several minutes. (GEPA Dec, 67)

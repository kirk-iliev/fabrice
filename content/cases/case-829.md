## Altona (Canada) — March 26, 1967 (2045)

Mr. and Mrs. John Dick and Mrs. W. Buhr were driving toward Gretna. Near three radio towers situated 35 km south of Altona, they saw a very bright object at ground level, illuminating the countryside with a pink glow. The object itself was dark, with a luminous band around it, was mo- tionless and pulsated, then suddenly disappeared. (173)

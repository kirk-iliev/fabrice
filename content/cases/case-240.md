## Montbazcns (France) — October 11, 1954 (2200)

A round craft, 4 m diameter, landed in a pasture. It gave off a powerful red light, took off with a formidable acceleration when wit- nesses (garage owner Mr. Carriere and son; Mr. Gardelle, farmer, Mr. Ginestre, shoemaker, and two others) came close to it. Mr. Gardelle felt "an electric shock." (60, 61; M 162)

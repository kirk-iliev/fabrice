## Languedoc, France: Aerial combat — 1395

### "In the land of Languedoc, a big star and five small ones were seen in the sky. These, as it

seemed, attacked and sought to fight the big one, which they followed for half an hour. Also a voice was heard in the sky, shouting. Then a man was seen, who seemed to be made of copper, holding a spear in his hand, and throwing fire. He grabbed the big star and hit it; after which, nothing more was seen."

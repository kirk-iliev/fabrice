## Monza (Italy) — November 8, 1954 (1030)

A crowd of about 150 people, destroy- ing barriers to come closer, gathered to observe a lumi- nous craft landed in a stadium. The object rested on three legs and had a dome emitting a blinding white light and supporting an antenna. Two small figures dressed in white and gray, wearing transparent hel- mets, were seen. They spoke with guttural sounds. One of them had a black face with a sort of trunk. When a man sicced a boxer dog onto the dwarfs, the animal turned around and bit him. The object rose with a shrill sound and vanished rapidly. (Wilkins U238)

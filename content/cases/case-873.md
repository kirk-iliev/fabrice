## Maiquetia Airport (Venezuela) — August 26, 1967 (0200)

Esteban D. Cova, a private in the Marines, was relieved of duty at the air- port and returned to his quarters. Then he met an ugly dwarf, 1 m tall, with a huge head, bulgy eyes, and a body covered with "hairy stuff or wiry niclal," It made a deep whistling sound, and Cova felt a prick- ling sensation in his body. In perfect Spanish the creature said: "Won't you come with me? We need one human being." Cova fainted. (Lor. Ill 79)

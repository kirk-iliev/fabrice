## Blame Air Force Base (Washington) — January 12, 1965

A member of a night federal agency, who was driving toward the base, saw a low-flying object, 10 m in diameter, which avoided collision at the last moment. He got out of the car and observed it hovering for one min, then it flew off at high speed. The object was tracked on radar. The same night, a round, glowing object with a dome on top landed on a nearby farm. melting snow in a 10 m diameter circle. (NICAP Mar., 65)

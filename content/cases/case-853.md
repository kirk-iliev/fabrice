## duly 7,1967 Milan (Italy) — 1967 (2330)

Antonio Brambila and another man saw a glow coming from a disk, 6.5 m in diameter and 2.5 m high, with a dome on top and four telescopic legs, which emitted a strange vibration. (LDLN 91)

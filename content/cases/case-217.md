## Rinkerode (Germany) — October 9, 1954

Willi Hoge, a projectionist, evening saw four occupants of an object that had come to ground level 70 m from the road. He was returning home when he observed a blue light and thought an airplane had made an emergency landing. The object was cigar-shaped, and four men wearing rubber coveralls were working under it. They were about 12 m tall and had very large chests and oversized heads; their legs were short and thin. (47)

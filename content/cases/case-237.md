## Lavarande (Algeria) — October 11, 1954 (0500)

A large disk flying very low over a road scared two truck drivers, Messrs. Gaston Breau and Amoura, who stopped their gasoline truck and ran away into the fields while the object flew on to- ward Medea in silence. (59)

## Lexisburg (Indiana) — March 30, 1966 (2035)

A civilian woman and her four children observed an oval object crossing the road as they were driving south about 15 km north of Lexis- burg. It came close to the car, and a pulsating sound was heard, increasing in frequency as the object came nearer, but it seemed to come through the car radio rather than directly from the object. The witness drove away in fear, but was followed for 13 km by the object, whose color suddenly changed from reddish- orange to bluish-white before accelerating out of sight. (Atic)

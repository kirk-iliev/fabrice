## Lake County (Ohio) — November 6, 1957 (0630)

A civilian source reported an object so bright that his eyes could not sustain it. It appeared to land on a ridge, then took off again. It was round and much larger than a plane, had an "odd color," left no trail and made no noise. (Atic)

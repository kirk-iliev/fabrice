## Livorno (Italy) — October 19, 1954

Bruno Senesi saw two shining ob- jects emitting smoke land in a field. Out of them came small, red, monstrous beings who chased him. In a state of great excitement Senesi was brought to a hospital, where he tried to hide under a bed, scream- ing and trembling in terror. (85)

## Becar, near Diges and "Les Michauts" or "Les Joli- vets" (France) — September 24, 1954 (0900)

Two women (Widow Gcoffroy and Miss Giselc Fin) made independent reports of a dark gray disk, 6 m in diameter, 1 m high, seen in a clear- ing. A man of normal height was standing close to it. He wore dark clothes and a kind of cap. Miss Fin came within 30 m of the craft and stated the man was repairing it. Traces were found on the grass. (14; Carrouges 98)

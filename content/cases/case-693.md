## Exeter (New Hampshire) — September 3, 1965 (0200)

A young man and two police officers observed five lights in a rigid configura- tion moving over a field, sometimes fluttering to low altitude with a falling-leaf motion. The lights illumi- nated the countryside and flashed in sequence. (Fuller)

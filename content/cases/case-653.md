## Bairio Paraiso dos Barbeiros (Brazil) — July 12, 1965

Student Hum- berto Aranjo da Silva nearly hit a saucer-shaped craft, 6 m in diameter, with two V-shaped landing pads and a dome, which had landed on the road. It made a whirring noise. (L.DLN 84, SBEDV 51, 53)

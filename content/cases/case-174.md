## La Roulerie (France) — October 1, 1954 (2000)

Near Saint Jean d’Angely, two businessmen, Messrs. Estier and Phelippeau, who were driving back from Royan, saw a little man cross- ing the road in front of their car. Having stopped, they saw the figure disappear into the woods. (28)

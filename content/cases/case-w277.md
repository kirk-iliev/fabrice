## Finis Terrae Cape, Galicia, Spain — 1729

### Strangers from the sky

A local story claims that three men came out of a cloud, had a meal at the market, took off and flew south.

**Source:** Benito Jeronimo Feijoo, Teatro critico universal (1726-1740), Volume Three (1729). Text from the Madrid edition of 1777, 86-87.

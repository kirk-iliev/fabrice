## Mendiozaca (Argentina) — June 14, 1968 (0300)

Jorge Yaru, 35, saw a strange light outside and a bright object, the size of a bus, 30 m away. It had yellow, green and red lights at the bottom. As he ajuproached, the object rose sud- denly, hovered for 40 min, and left at high speed. (LDLN 95)

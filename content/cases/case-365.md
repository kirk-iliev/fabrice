## Stockton (Georgia) — July 3, 1955

Mrs. Wesley Symmonds was driving near this town when she saw four "bug-eyed” creatures near the road. They were small beings with thin arms, large eyes, and pointed chins. Two were turned away from the witness; one was bending over with something like a stick in its hand; and the fourth one was facing her with its right arm raised. It had bulging eyes, a sort of cap, no visible mouth, a long pointed nose, a chin which came to a sharp point, and long thin arms with claws. (Stringficld; Humanoids 54)

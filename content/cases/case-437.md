## Holly (West Virginia) — November 8, 1957 (1430)

Hank Mollohan and eight other persons saw an elongated object, 12 m long, with several portholes from which fire and smoke appeared to be coming. It swung at low altitude and 266 PASSPOET TO MAGONIA dropped to the ground. People seemed to be moving around it, but the witnesses were driven away by a hard rain. (M 265)

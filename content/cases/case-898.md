## Storrington (Great Britain) — November 16, 1967 (1700)

Mrs. Quick and another woman saw a flickering light and a deep-red, glowing object at ground level, with a dark figure, slightly- larger than a normal human, approaching it. The object disappeared suddenly. (FSR 68, 2)

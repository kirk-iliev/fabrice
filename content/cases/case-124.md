## Todd River Downs (Australia) — 1954

A native was riding a horse over a low ridge when a spherical object about 13 m in diameter "nearly skittled me off my horse.” t went up suddenly with a heavy wind, rumbling and emitting four columns of smoke. (Wilkins U 211)

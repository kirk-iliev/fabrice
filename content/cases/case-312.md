## Les Metairies (France) — October 26, 1954 (2030)

Approximate date. At this place situated near Saint Quirin, Mrs. Louis and her sons Mare and Yvon observed an orange craft, 6 m diameter and 3 m high, fly over them as their tractor engine died. The lights also went out, and the battery was found dead after the sighting. (Personal)

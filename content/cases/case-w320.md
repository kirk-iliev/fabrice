## Bucharest, Romania — 28 December 1793

### Unexplained "moon"

In the evening, a man who was dining in Bucharest about 7:30 P.M. reported that "the moon has accomplished a miracle," making a journey along the sky in half an hour.

**Source:** Ion Hobana and Julien Weverbergh, Les Ovni en URSS et dans les Pays de I'Est (Paris: Robert Laffont, 1972), 222. The authors cite Biblioteca Academiei Romane, BAR ms. rom. 2150, fol. 1110.

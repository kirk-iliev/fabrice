## Newfield (New York) — October 24, 1967 (2130)

Newfield {New York). Donald Chiszar, 13, and Pat Crozier, 10, saw a bright object hovering in mid-air. It tilted toward them, and they could see two big, square windows separated by a bar, and strange letter- ing under them. Two aliens, and control panels with lights and knobs, could be seen inside. It vanished on the spot. (Lor. Ill, 180}

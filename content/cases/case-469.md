## Leman Lake (Switzerland) — August 16, 1958

Leman Lake (Switzerland), A dozen people out on 1700 the lake in perfect weather saw a bright light coming down. They stopped their boat as it came to hover about 15 m above them. It was saucer-shaped, 10 m in diameter, with a cabin showing several windows on top. The outer disk below the cabin was spinning. As it came down toward the water, a noticeable cur- rent was created. No noise or occupants were noticed. After several leaps in mid-air, the craft flew off at “unbelievable” speed. (Personal)

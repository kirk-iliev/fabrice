## Olesa de Montserrat, Catalonia, Spain — 3 July 1642

### Globe, changing its appearance

Joseph Aguilera and others saw a globe changing to "three moons," later an enormous light seen for one hour.

**Source:** Guijarro, Josep, Guia de la Cataluha Magica (Barcelona: Ediciones Martinez Roca, 1999), 48.

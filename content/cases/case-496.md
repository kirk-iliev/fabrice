## Werdehl-Eveking (Germany) — August 25, 1959 (1100)

Near Hagen, Lutz Holtmann went toward a bright object in a forest, and fainted when he got close to it. When he re- gained consciousness, he saw it take off silently and vertically. It was round, had a tripod landing gear, two rows of bright openings, and was about 30 m in diameter. (Nachrichten Oct., 59)

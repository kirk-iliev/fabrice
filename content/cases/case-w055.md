## Mount Nyoigadake, Japan — 1185

### A luminous wheel enters the sea

104. First there were red beams behind the mountain, and then an object like a luminous wheel flew over, and entered the sea. The witnesses were fishermen.

**Source:** Brothers Magazine III, 1, 1964. 105.

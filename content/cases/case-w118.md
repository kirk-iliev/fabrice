## Moldavia, Romania — 8 November 1517

### An object resembling a face

According to the 17 century Moldavian chronicler, Grigore Ureche (ca. 1590-1617), "a great blue sign shining like the face of a man" appeared in the sky. After some time without moving, it "hid itself in the sky again." th

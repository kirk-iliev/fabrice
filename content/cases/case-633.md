## Williamsburg (Virginia) — January 23, 1965 (9840)

A 31-year-old man driving a '64 Cadillac was at the intersection of U.S. Highway 60 and State Route 14 when the engine failed, and he had to stop by the side of the road. lie then observed an object about 12 in above ground. It was shaped like a mushroom or an electric bulb, 25 m high, 8 m in diameter, made noise similar to a vacuum cleaner, had a metallic gray color, a red-orange light on one side, and a blue one on the other. It took off against the wind toward the west, at high speed. (Atic)

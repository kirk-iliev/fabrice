## Saint-Point Lake (France) — October 18, 1954 (2245)

Miss Bourriot saw a bright red light on Route N437 near the old factory, Near it were three beings: Iwo, dwarfs, crossed the road ahead of her; the third was a man of medium size. The craft took off, flying over the lake at high speed. (Quincy; M 197; LDLN 97; Magonia)

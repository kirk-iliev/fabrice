## Iena, Germany — 13 June 1554

### Spheres and disks

192. ^ A large number of spheres and disks flew over the city of Iena. They had sudden variations of speed and turned to a red color as they flew north.

**Source:** Mathias Miles, Siebenbiirgischer Wiirgenengel (Hermannstadt, 1670).

## June, 1968 Carlos Paz (Argentina) — Unknown date (0050)

Pedro Pretzel, 39, motel owner, observed an object 50 m away on road 20, showing two powerful red headlights. Arriving at his room, the witness found his daughter unconscious. When she came to, she said that a blond man, 2 m tall, wearing a blue, bright suit, and holding a pale- blue sphere in his hand, had appeared and spoken to her. (LDLN95)

## Jcttingen (France) — October 7, 1954

A railroad employee, Rene Ott, dawn saw a mushroom-shaped object 3 m in diameter, in a field 3 m away from Route N16 and 1 m above ground. A luminous rectangle, like a door, was seen on the side. It took off and flew about 5 m above the witness, following him to the next village. (41; M 143)

## Friesland, Holland — 1182

### Four suns, armed men in the sky

103. The Chronicler Winsemius (1622) reports, in his Croniek van Vriesland that four Suns and a score of armed men were seen in the sky and a bloody rain fell.

**Source:** M.D. Teenstra, Volksverhalen en Legenden van vroegere en latere dagen (Geertsema: Groningen, 1843), 117.

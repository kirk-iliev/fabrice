## Saint George (Utah) — May 11, 1967 (0200)

Michael Campeadorc, 25, a hospital employee, was driving to Salt Lake City when he heard a noise similar to that of a truck, but saw nothing. Then a yellow light became visible to the left, and thinking that it was a jet plane about to crash, he got out and saw it stop 30 m away at 30 m altitude. It was shaped like an inverted bowl with a dome on top and looked metallic. The witness became afraid and emptied his .25 Beretta in the direction of the craft. He heard the bullets hit metal and the object took off at great speed. When he told his story 2 a nearby service station, the attendant replied that 2 bout 20 reports had been made in the Saint George area recently. (180)

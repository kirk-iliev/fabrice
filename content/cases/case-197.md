## Le Mans (France) — October 5, 1954 (0630)

East of Le Mans on Route N23. Renault employees were going to work when they saw near the road a luminous object on the ground and felt "pricklings and a sort of paralysis." The ob- ject emitted a burst of green light and flew away very low over the fields. (42; Vuillequez; M 143)

## Bradford (England) — August 16, 1955 (0400)

Mr. Ernest Suddard, 35, and his 13-year-old son were in a lorry on Roundhill Street when they saw what seemed to be a human being about 1.20 m tall. dressed in skintight black clothes. It held its arms close to its sides, its feet close together, and walked by a series of jumps. On its chest was a silver disk perforated witli holes. It turned off sud- denly into a passageway and was lost to sight. The witnesses were too amazed to follow it. (Constance 222)

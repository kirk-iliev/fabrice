## Epping (Great Britain) — December 27, 1963 (1600)

A shiny white object was seen on the ground at Bank's Stables. It was about 3.5 m long, | m high, and had something like a wind- shield more brilliant than the rest of the craft. It took off, flew horizontally for 30 m, and was hidden from view. Grass was flattened over a circular area, and four traces were found. (BUFORA 1)

## Pasnembi (Brazil) — July 30, 1962

A man driving near Pasnembi stopped when his engine failed and observed a cylindrical object, described as "a bottle with two necks,” about 40 m long, 15m high, which had landed on the road. Two men (one at either end of the craft) appeared to be changing some luminous signals. This lasted 10 min and the craft took off at high speed. In Alta, a number of people reported a bright object moving at high altitude. (136; FSR 62, 6)

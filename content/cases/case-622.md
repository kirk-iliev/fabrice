## Lynn (Massachusetts) — August 25, 1964 (2230)

Richard Pratt, 17, heard a whistling noise and saw a silvery, oval object sur- rounded with a soft white glow, supporting a dome, which went down to ground level. A similar object had been observed one hour earlier at Littleton, about 50 km west, by four boys. It was described as silvery gray, with three blinking red lights and a white light. (NICAP Sept., 64)

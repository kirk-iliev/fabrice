## Tangier (Oklahoma) — April 1, 1966 (2240)

A civilian man, 34, was driving about 10 km south of Tangier when he reached a hill- top and saw a green object flying north at very high speed, emitting a shrieking noise and a "heat wave.” The car engine died. The witness noted that the ob- ject was wider than the road. (Atic)

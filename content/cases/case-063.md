## Raveo (Italy) — August 14, 1947 (0900)

R. L. Johatmis saw a disk on the ground and two dwarfs less than 1 m tall, wearing dark blue coveralls with red collars and belts. They had over- sized heads, greenish faces, huge, salient dark eyes without eyelashes or eyebrows, but surrounded by a ringlike muscle. They wore something similar to crash helmets. The center of their belts projected a "vapor," and the witness suffocated, feeling a strong electrical discharge. The creatures had greenish hands with eight talonlike fingers. (Humanoids 2; Magonia; FRS 67,1)

## Montsoreau (France) — July 28, 1966

A photographer, Mr. Lacoste, evening and his wife, saw a red, lighted object cross the sky and appear to touch the ground. It then rose, hovered, and disappeared. The next day a wheat field was found flattened over an area 3 m in diameter, and covered with an oily substance. (166, Magonia)

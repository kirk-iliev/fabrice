## Gulf of Guinea — October 28, 1902 (0305)

Three persons aboard the "Fort Salis- bury." including Second Officer A. H. Raymer, saw a huge, dark object bearing lights in the sea ahead. It was observed sinking slowly. Estimated length: 200 m. (Fort 642; Anatomy 20)

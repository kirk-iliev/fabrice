## Botucatu (Brazil) — July 1, 1968 (1230)

Three boys saw an object, about 8 m wide, 350 m away. It had a large tripod under- carriage and a ladder reaching down to the ground. These devices were retracted, and the object rose a few meters, then flew off at high speed. (FSR 68, 6)

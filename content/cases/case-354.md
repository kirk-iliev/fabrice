## San Carlos (Venezuela) — December 16, 1954

Three young men saw a dwarf, who attacked one of them, Jesus Paz, before fleeing into a disk-shaped machine, which flew off immediately. It was flat and shiny and had been hovering 1 m above ground. (Lor. I 50, Humanoids 35)

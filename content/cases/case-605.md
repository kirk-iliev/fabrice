## Massillon (Ohio) — May 17, 1964 (2100)

A fiery object, maneuvering ;it low altitude, was seen from Wooster and Smithville at 2110, then from Lawrence and Burbank between 2125 and 2130. It flew erratically with a whirring sound and changes of color, apparently interfering with police radio. It went down toward the northwest, seemingly ready to land. Abnormal radioactivity al- legedly was found at the site. (Akron UFO Res. Soc.)

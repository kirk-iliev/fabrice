## Bonn, Germany — 11 October 1847

### Fast-moving intruder

438. Single object, a small black spot rapidly crossing the disk of the sun, reported by astronomer Schmidt. It was "neither a bird nor an insect crossing before the telescope."

**Source:** R. C. Carrington, Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society 20 (January 1860): 100-1. 439.
**Case:** W392

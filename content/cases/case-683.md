## Cuzco (Peru) — August 20, 1965 (1150)

Several tourists, including Alberto Ugarte and Elwin Voter, observed a strange craft landing near the Inca ruins that they were visiting. Two creatures described as luminous dwarfs, who seemed to have "vertical mouths,” were seen briefly. (Humanoids 43)

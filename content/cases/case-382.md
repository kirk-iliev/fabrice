## Jacksonville (Florida) — May 9, 1956 (2300)

Two girls, Joan Frost and Gertie Wynn, while waiting for a bus, saw two pulsating lights flying horizontally and disappearing. 15 min later the lights were seen again, stopping at the zenith. merging, and diving to 50 m altitude. As the bus arrived, it seemed that the objects had sepa- rated and were about to abduct the witnesses, and they ran to the vehicle in terror. (APRO Mar., 59)

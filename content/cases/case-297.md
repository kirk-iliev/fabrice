## Melito (Italy) — October 21, 1954

A young man walking in a field heard a rustling noise and saw a strange craft land nearby. Getting closer to investigate, he saw a pilot with a diving suit coming out of the craft, which emitted bronze-green rays of light, flooding the whole country- side. The witness was paralyzed. A dog barking about 100 m away caused the rapid escape of the pilot into the craft, which took off. (88)

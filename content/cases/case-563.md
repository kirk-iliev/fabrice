## Willow Grove (Australia) — February 15, 1963 (0710)

A civilian observed an object arriving from the east. It stopped at 15 m alti- tude over his farmhouse. The object made a swishing sound, and measured about 8 m in diameter. 3 m in height. The underside was spinning in a counter- clockwise direction, had a blue color and no light. It took off faster than a jet, after hovering about five sec. The witness suffered from a strong headache all day. (Atic)

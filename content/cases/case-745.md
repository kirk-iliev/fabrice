## Mar — March 31, 1966 (2115)

31.1966 Hamilton (Canada). Charles Kozens, 13, saw two objects, 2.5 m long, 12 m high, with flashing lights, land near Hamilton. When he tried to touch an antenna on one of the objects, he received an electric shock. (FSR 66, 4)

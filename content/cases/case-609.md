## Learn Lane (England) — June 2, 1964 (1730)

David Wilson, 14, was going to get some straw for his rabbits when he stopped with other children to observe half-a-dozen dwarfs, about 80 cm tall, dressed in bright green, and having hands lighted "like electric bulbs," who seemed to be searching for something. Another child later re- ported that she had seen a silvery disk-shaped object, he size of a car, take off from the same location. (FS Dec, 64)

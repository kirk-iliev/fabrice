## Boston Creek (Canada) — August 27, 1954 (2030)

A wingless flying object was seen by Bill Supa. an employee of the Caswell Con- struction Co. It landed about 2 km way from the witness, who approached within 300 m before it took off and flew away. Grass was flattened where it had landed. (Wilkins U 227)

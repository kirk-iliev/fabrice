## Farm Hill (Australia) — January 13, 1958 (2345)

Brian Crittendon, 21, was chased by a dome-shaped object that emitted a nar- row light beam toward the ground. He was so fright- ened that he drove home on a half-flat tire, followed for 5 km by the object, which was about 50 m away and 10 m above ground. It overtook his car at a speed exceeding 100 km/h. Radio interference was noted, but no noise. (UFO Bulletin Mar., 58)

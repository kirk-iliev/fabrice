## Fayetteville (Tennessee) — March 28, 1966 (2000)

A man driving at a speed of about 100 km/h suddenly encountered a large ighted object 1 m above the road on a hilltop. It flew off, as the car engine and headlights died. The light bulbs had to be replaced. The object was oval, 7 m ong, dark gray, and showed about 30 lights along its periphery. (Atic)

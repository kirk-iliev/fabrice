## Edinburgh (Scotland) — November 8, 1957

Fourteen people, including Mrs. Maty Home, reported to police that a disk- shaped object followed their truck, dived toward it, came within 20 m of them, then left toward the sea, eaving a double vapor trail. (Round-up 217; 116)

## Millinocket (Maine) — May 24, 1964 (2100)

A man driving on Millinocket Lake Road saw a fiery, spherical object to the side. He stopped and left his car with a friend to ob- serve it better. When they became afraid and walked back to the car, the sphere followed them: the engine could not be started as long as the sphere remained within 2 or 3 m of the car. It flew away after five min. It was described as a ball of fire without structure, about 75 cm in diameter. (Atic)

## Recife (Venezuela) — August 4, 1967

Engineer Hugo S. Yepcs was on early a beach 25 km north of Recife when he saw a disk, 6 m in diameter, come out of the water. Il was gray, looked metallic. hovered fora few sec al I m altitude, then rose slowly to the east and disappeared. (NICAP Mar., 68)

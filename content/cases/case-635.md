## South Brighton (New Zealand) — February 3, 1965 (2045)

A man saw a light on the beach near Penguin Street and got out of his car to observe it. He then heard a modulated whistling sound and saw an object, 7 m wide, rise from the beach to an altitude of about 20 m. He came back to he site with other persons, and a dog that became testless at a spot where grass was found flattened. Another witness, driving near Humphrey Ave., saw he object as it rose over South Brighton. (149)

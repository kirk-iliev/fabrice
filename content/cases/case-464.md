## Maceio (Brazil) — 1958 (0600)

Near Paripueira, jeweler Wilson Lustosa and numerous fishermen saw an object hover- ing 15m above the sea, 40 m away. It was lens-shaped, about 12 m thick, and showed portholes with a glow- ing red light. Below the object the water seemed to be "poiling” or attracted upward, while a soft, whirring sound was heard. For one hour the object kept going up and down. (SBEDV)

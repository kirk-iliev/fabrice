## Glencoe (Australia) — May 20, 1963

A 17-year-old witness, who evening wishes anonymity for fear of ridicule, saw what he first thought was a bulldozer by the side of the road. He came within 20 m of it, then was blinded by a strong light as his car suddenly stopped. The object crossed the road and flew away. The light was as intense as that of a welder’s torch. The witness's father testified that his son came home white and visibly terrified. (APRO Nov., 63)

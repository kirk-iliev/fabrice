## Cosenza, Italy — 6 October 1835

### Maneuvering pyramid

416. A pyramid-shaped meteor appears and heads off towards a mountain, leaving a "gloomy tail." It first appeared as a lighted object seen flying West of Cosenza. It rose into the air and changed shape, leaving a vaporous trail, moving slowly towards the south. It followed a parabolic curve and disappeared towards Fiumefreddo harbor.

**Source:** "Casistica dei Fenomeni Straordinari" in Orizonti Sconosciuti 5, "Periodo: 1819-1857" (1976); Nicola Leoni, Delia Magna Grecia e delle tre Calabrie (Napoli, 1844), 325-326.

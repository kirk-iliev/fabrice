## Vereeniging (South Africa) — December 14, 1963 (0100)

Messrs. Muller and Immelman suddenly found the countryside illumi- nated and saw an object, 15 m in diameter, with in- tense orange and blue lights, emitting sparks, flying toward their car. They stopped and jumped out as it dived five or six times, at one point hovering for two min 15m above them, making a humming sound, be- fore flying away. (146, FSR 64, 3)

## Herman (Minnesota) — December 20, 1965

Edward Burnd, 15, was driv- ing west in a farm truck when he saw an object 2 m above the road about 30 m away. As the engine died, the object glowed red and the witness lost conscious- ness. When he awoke, the truck was facing east and rested in a ditch: (FSR 66, 6)

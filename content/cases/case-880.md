## Villa Constitucion (Argentina) — September 11, 1967 (2130)

Villa Constitucion (Argentina), During a raging storm, an entire family watched a large. glowing ob- ject in a field 300 m away, giving off brilliant beams of light for 4 hours, after which it took off in seconds. Sootlike material with an unpleasant smell and tracks on flattened grass were noted. (FSR 68, 6)

## Lorerami Valley (Argentina) — July 15, 1965

Mr. and Mrs. Bosquets and their family observed a luminous object, its color changing from blue to orange, about 12 m in diam- eter, and showing several portholes. The object left traces on the ground. (CODOVNI 1965)

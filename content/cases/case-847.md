## Richmond (Virginia) — June 1, 1967 (2330)

John Norton, 7, had to be taken to a hospital after observing a disk-shaped, buzzing object, the size of a Volkswagen, with green and red lights, which landed in a clearing and took of "like lightning." (NICAP May, 67)

## Itajuba (Brazil) — June 7, 1967 (0130)

A vehicle stalled in the vicinity of a brilliant, transparent, mushroom-shaped object, aboard which occupants with catlike faces were seen. Under the object was a quadrangular protuberance. (FSR 68, 6)

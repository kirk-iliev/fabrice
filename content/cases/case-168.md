## Isle of Re (France) — September 30, 1954

As he was returning home near . 2200 La Flotte-en-Re, Celeste Simonutti saw a bright light and, fearing a fire, rushed to the scene. There he observed a luminous sphere 12 m in diameter hover- ing at 1 m altitude, turning red, blue, and taking off. Two other witnesses. (28)

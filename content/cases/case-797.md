## Stirum (North Dakota) — September 13, 1966 (0730)

A child of 11 years saw a object land near a farm. It had a tripod landing gear, two red lights, two white and one green light, and a transparent dome. A businessman from Gwinner and an Air Force Lt. Col. went to the site and discovered three traces, apparently left by spher- ical objects, 18 cm deep, very compact. Radiation measured at 0.1 millirocnlgen (normal). The child reported that the object “went away so fast that it vanished.” (Atic)

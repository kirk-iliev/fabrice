## Bouchard (Argentina} — June 25, 1968 (1045)

June 25,1968 Bouchard (Argentina}. Jorge Ribles, electrician at the Carmal Chemical Plant, noticed that the engines were at a standstill, while the countryside was lit up with intensive reddish light. Another man, 52-year- old Jose J. Rociski, fell unconscious as a vivid light enveloped him. He was unable to move his left arm for three days, and suffered from pain and extreme nervousness. (FSR 68, 5)

## Durhamville (New York) — April 5, 1966 (0130)

Durhamville {New York). A woman was awakened by a flash, thought her heater had exploded, but found everything in order. The next day she was told that a pulsating, luminous object had flown at very low altitude over her trailer, circled and left. Three wit- nesses. Low buzzing sound. (Binder)

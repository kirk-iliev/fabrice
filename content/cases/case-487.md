## Grassy Plains (Canada) — April 29, 1959 (2230)

Alex Gillis and Jerry Monk- man saw from a hilltop an egg-shaped object in the middle of the road. It was about 5 m long, the top part emitting a bright light. The object went away silently. Fearing ridicule, the witnesses reported the incident one month later. (FSR 59, 6) 276 PASSPORT TO MACONIA

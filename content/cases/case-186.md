## Ronsenac (France) — October 3, 1954 (2245)

A circular craft was seen by Jean Allary between Montmoreau and Villebois-Lavalette. It seemed to be gliding on the ground. It showed luminous spots and became completely illuminated when it took off. It was about 120 m high. Grass was ound flattened and scorched over an area 7 m across. (36;M 130)

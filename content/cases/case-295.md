## Parravicino d'Erba (Italy) — October 20, 1954

Renzo Pugina, 37, had night just put his car in the garage when he saw a strange being covered with a "scaly" luminous suit, about 13 m tall, standing near a tree. The creature aimed the beam from a sort of flashlight at him, and he felt paralyzed, until a motion he made when clenching his fist on the garage keys seemed to free him. He attacked the intruder, who rose and fled with a soft whirring sound. An oily spot was found at the site. (77, Magonia)

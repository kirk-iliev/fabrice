## San Casciano (Italy) — April 10, 1962

Mario Zuccula, 27, was walk- evening ing home when he suddenly felt a current of cold air. He was paralyzed with fear when he saw a white ob- ject, 10 m in diameter, metallic in appearance, which hovered close to the ground. A metallic cylinder was lowered from the craft, and two little men, about 1.30 m tall, came out through a door lighted by a source inside the cylinder. A low voice similar to the sound of an electronic device told him they would return at the end of the fourth moon, one hour before dawn, with a message. The beings had their heads covered with a hood. (FSR 62, 4)

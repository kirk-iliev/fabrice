## Brooksville (Florida) — March 2, 1965 (1355)

John F. Reeves, 65, retired, was walking in the woods when he observed an object 10 m in diameter, 2 m thick, saucer-shaped, with an outer rim and a stairway. After watching it for 10 min, he saw a robotlike being, about 1.30 m tall, wearing a silver uniform, glass headgear, and thin, white gloves. The being walked to the craft, then returned with a box that emitted a flash when pointed at the witness. The object subsequently took off with a whistling sound. (Atic)

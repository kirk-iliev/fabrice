## Hubbard (Oregon) — May 18, 1964

A bright, silvery object, 3 m long. 15 m high, with a cone-shaped front part, resting on four legs, was seen in a wheat field by 10-year-old Mike Bizon. It made a beeping noise. rose first to the altitude of the telephone poles, then took off ver- tically. Wheat was found flattened in all directions. (NICAP July, 64)

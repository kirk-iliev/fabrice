## Hilliards (Ohio) — February 5, 1967

A young man heard a strange noise evening and a barking dog, and saw an egg-shaped object land. From an elevatorlike shaft came human figures that placed small spheres around the craft. A man walked to them and appeared to speak with the entities. The witness was seen, the creatures tried to abduct him, and then took off {NICAP May, 67)

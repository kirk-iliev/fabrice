## Ulysses (Oklahoma) — September 11, 1964 (0600)

Karen Campbell was scared by an oval, dull copper object that flew low over her car. t measured about 1.2 m in height, had a dome on top, made a "rushing" noise, and seemed to "float" over the car. (148)

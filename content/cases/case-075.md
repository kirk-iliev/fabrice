## Kokomo (Indiana) — April 8, 1950 (0200)

A metal worker was awakened by his dog and observed an object 60 m away at low altitude. It was a gray metal disk. 5 m in diameter, shaped like a top with a kind of turret. It was oscil- lating, spinning slowly, and had three portholes shin- ing with a blue-white light. It hovered for about 2 min, left toward the north, very fast. (Atic)

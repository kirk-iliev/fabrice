## Kitchener (Canada) — April 26, 1967 (2100)

Brian Dorscht saw a dark object, measuring | m in diameter and 15 m in height, with flashing green and white lights. When 1 m above ground, six legs came out, and it landed on a lawn near the National Grocers Company. The whining sound the object was making stopped, and the wit- ness became afraid and ran away. Police found six prints at the site. (177; SS&S Summer, 67)

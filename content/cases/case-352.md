## Chico (Venezuela) — December 10, 1954

Having seen a bright object land evening near the Trans-Andean Highway, two young men ap- proached it, found it was shaped like two bowls glued together, about 3 m diameter. The underside was a source of fiery light. Four small beings emerged and attacked them, in an apparent kidnapping attempt. The dwarfs were extremely strong, their bodies cov- ered with hair. They fled into the machine, and it took off, (Lor. I 51)

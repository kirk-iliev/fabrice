## Hoganas (Sweden) — December 20, 1958 (0255)

Near Domsten, Hans Gustavs- son, 35, and Stig Rydberg, 30, saw something in the woods and stopped their car to observe it. They found a disk about 5 m wide set on a tripod. All of a sudden they were attacked by four gray-colored creatures de- scribed as "fluid," but one witness reached the car and blew the horn for help. The beings fled and the craft took off, emitting "paralyzing vibrations." (Lor. II 56)

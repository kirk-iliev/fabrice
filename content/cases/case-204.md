## Plozevet (France) — October 7, 1954

The crew of several fishing boats early saw a luminous, orange-colored object over the coast, apparently surrounded by dense smoke. Villagers saw the same object and stated independently that it came within 10 m of the ground, then flew away to the southeast. (41; M 140)

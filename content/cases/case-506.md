## Siracusa (Italy) — May 19, 1960

Salvatore Cianci, jeweler, and his night wife, were driving near this Sicilian town when a creature about | m tall appeared in the headlights. It wore a shining coverall and a diving helmet and had two short wings. Mrs. Cianci suffered from shock. (FSR 64, 4)

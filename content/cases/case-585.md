## Daylston (Australia) — October 31, 1963 (0415)

Jim Davidson saw an object about 3 m long, bearing orange and red lights, which came close to his light truck, flew ahead of him, then departed and appeared to land behind a hill. Two witnesses independently reported a maneuvering light. (Austr. FSR May,’ 64)

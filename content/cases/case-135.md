## Hemmingford (Canada) — August 10, 1954 (2130)

The Coupal children said that a brightly lighted object followed them to the farm, Mr, Coupal and his oldest son went to the field where the children had been playing and saw an orange object rise and speed off to the west. Grass was flattened over 15 m, with two tracks about 5 m long. (Wilkins U 237)

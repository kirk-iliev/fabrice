## Evillers (France) — May 22, 1967

A teacher, 45-year-old Mr. Tyrode, evening saw a glow, then an object shaped like an inverted plate emitting a greenish-blue light. It flew less than 20 m above his car, at 40 km/h. (LDLN 94)

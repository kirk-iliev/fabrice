## Criteuil-la-Madeleine (France) — October 21, 1954 (2000)

The car driven by a bricklayer, Mr. Fillonneau, stoped as a large ball of ire flew near it. A violent air displacement was felt. "The battery was dead and the headlights were burned out." Thorough police investigation failed to identify the cause of the phenomenon. (79)

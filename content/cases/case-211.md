## Teheran (Iran) — October 8, 1954 (0230)

Ghaseme Fili, of Amiteah Street, was on the second floor of his house when he saw a luminous, white flying object stop in mid-air 20 m away. Lights were shining from the rear and the sides of the craft, inside which could be seen a small man dressed in black, wearing a mask with a trunk like an elephant. "I was standing with both hands on the bar of my balcony, looking with astonishment at this strange object, when I suddenly felt as though I were being drawn up toward the object by a magnet." Mr. Fili cried out in terror and woke up his neighbors. The object shot straight up, emitting sparks, and it was lost to sight almost immediately. (52, 58)

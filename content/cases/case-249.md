## Bourrasole (France) — October 13, 1954 (1935)

Messrs. Olivier and Perano and a third man saw reddish disk about 4 diameter a m with a small being close by, about 1.2 m tall, wearing a diving suit: "His head was large with respect to the rest of the body and he had two enormous eyes. The suit was bright and shiny like glass." The craft was surrounded by a sort of misty glow. One of the men came within 20 m of it and found himself paralyzed. The craft took off, throwing him to the ground, and rose very fast. (62, 63)

## Cyrus (Minnesota) — November 16, 1965

T. Untiedt was driving on High way 28 about 5 km west of Cyrus when he saw a red fluorescent, cylindrical object with a cone on top, 4m long and 2 m in diameter, surrounded with a white glow. The car slowed down as the observer was 400 m away from the object, which took off toward the northeast. (FSR 66, 6)

## Case 220 — October 9, 1954 (1830)

Pournoy-la-Chetive (France), Four children—Gilbert Calda, 12; Daniel Hirsch, 9; J. P. Hirsch, 5; and Rob- ert Maguin, 16—were roller-skating when they saw a luminous object near the cemetery. It was round, about 2.5 m in diameter, standing on three legs. A dwarf, about 1.2 m tall, dressed in black, having a face covered with hair and large eyes, came out and shone a blinding light at them, and said something in an unknown language. The children ran away, but looked back in time to see the object flying away high in the sky. (49; M 154; Magonia)

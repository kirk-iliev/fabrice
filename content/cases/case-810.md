## Freetown (Indiana) — January 17, 1967

F. Bedel, 23, driving on Route night 135 about 8 km north of Freetown saw a plate-shaped object with red. yellow, blue, and white blinking lights, lost control of his car, and ran off the road. Less than 2 km away, Phil Patton saw an identical object that came within 30 m of his car. (Lor. II] 22) APPENDIX Co]

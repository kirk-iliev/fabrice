## Summer, 1952 Itenhaem (Brazil) — 1952 (0300)

A woman was awakened by a thunderclap and a strong bluish light. As she got out, she saw a large number of hovering disk-shaped ma- chines resembling "inverted soup plates” 200 m away at an altitude of about 1 m. She observed them for 30 min, saw two figures standing on one of the craft and looking at the sky. They went back inside, and shortly thereafter the "fleet" took off, one object at a time. (FSR68, 1)

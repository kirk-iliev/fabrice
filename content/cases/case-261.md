## Perpignan (France) — October 15, 1954

Near the swimming pool in Saint-Assiscle, Damien Figucres, 56, was walking with his dogs when a reddish, luminous sphere landed 30 m away and an individual in a diving suit walked around it. The dogs barked at him. He boarded the machine, and it flew away in silence. (65; Carrouges 120)

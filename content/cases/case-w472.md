## Kent, Britain — Circa 523

### Weird sky phenomena, drops of blood

"In his time strange sights were seen of Dragons, Lions and other furious wild Beasts Fighting in the Air. In the West of Kent it Rained Wheat, and soon after great Drops of Blood, upon which ensued extream Dearth..." Scholar C. E. Britton comments: "Vaguely allocated to the reigh of Octa (ca. 513-533). Legendary." Here we have a further challenge, since nothing indicates that these events were correlated at all, or even happened at te same time. (Britton, C.E., A Meteorological Chronology to A.D. 1450, London: H.M.S.O., 1937.)

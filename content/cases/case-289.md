## Fabriano (Italy) — October 19, 1954 (2230)

Two men saw a disk-shaped object. 6 m diameter, hovering and emitting small violet flashes. On top of it was a very high antenna. Two small robotlike beings, 12 m tall. descended from it on a long ladder. Their eyes were "sharp" and dark red. They said something that sounded like "Dbano da skigyay o dbano,” went back aboard their craft, and flew away. (87)

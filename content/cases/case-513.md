## Carignan (France) — December 9, 1960 (2030)

A dog barked at a glowing object resting in a park. Three witnesses observed it from separate locations. It appeared as an oval, luminous craft, 4 m in diameter, inside which vague shadows were seen. It took off toward the north. A circle of yellowed grass was found at the site. (Ouranos 27)

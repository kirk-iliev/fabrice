## Saint-Germain-de-Livet (France) — October 10, 1954 (1400)

A math professor, Mr. Bon, observed a silvery disk about 7 m in diam- eter rise silently from a point about 250 m to the side of the road. Spinning, it dived to the ground from an altitude of about 800 m, then flew off horizontally at a dizzying speed. (55, 57)

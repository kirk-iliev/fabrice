## Floresta (Venezuela) — December 10, 1954 (1830)

A doctor from Caracas who was driving with his father near this town stopped his car as two little men were running into the bushes. Soon thereafter, a luminous disk rose from the side. of the road with a sizzling sound and flew away, (Lor. I 43)

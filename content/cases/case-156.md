## Chabeuil (France) — September 26, 1954 (1430)

Mrs. Leboeuf was suddenly con- fronted with a creature resembling "a child in a plastic bag, with eyes larger than human eyes." This creature entered a flat. circular machine, which took off toward the northeast with a soft whistling. Traces. Witnesses in state of shock. (17; Carrouges 116; Anatomy 70; Magonia)

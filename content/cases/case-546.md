## Overfield (Great Britain) — September 13, 1962 (2320)

Myra Jones was driving between Overfield and Norris Hill when she saw a luminous, gray object, larger than a car, with a dome on top. It was flying at the altitude of the telephone poles, slowly spinning. Dark spots were visible on the underside. It nearly touched the car, then gave a whistling sound and flew away. (137; FSR 62, 6)

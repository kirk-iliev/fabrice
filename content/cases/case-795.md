## Durand (Wisconsin) — September 7, 1966 (2000)

About 15 km cast of Durand, Mrs. E. Bruns and her two children observed an elongated object, about 10 m long, with revolving lights, hovering 1 m above ground and making a deafening noise. After 30 sec, it tilted and took off. (APROSept.,66)

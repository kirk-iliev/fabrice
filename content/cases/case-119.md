## Case 119 — October 25, 1953 (2130)

Santa Fe {New Mexico). Jim Milligan, 16, was driv- ing through a park when he saw something fall in front of his car and stopped as the object landed in some bushes. lie walked toward it, found a craft that looked like two ship hulls, about 3 m long, 2 m wide, glued together. When he tried to touch it, the object flew away. (Wilkins A 223)

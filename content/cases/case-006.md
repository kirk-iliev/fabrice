## Lamy (New Mexico) — March 26, 1880

Four men walking near Galis- evening teo Junction were surprised as they heard voices com- ing from a "strange balloon," which flew, over them. It was shaped like a fish and seemed to be guided by a large fanlike device. There were eight to ten figures aboard. Their language was not understood. The ob- ject flew low over Galisteo Junction and rose rapidly toward the east. (FSR 65, 3)

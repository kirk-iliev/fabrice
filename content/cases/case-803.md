## El Campo (Texas) — November 2, 1966

Mrs. Mark deFriend, 32, saw an evening object at ground level in front of her car on a rainy night. It would leave the road and fly over the fields from time to time. The greenish-blue object flew back in front of the car, then was lost to sight behind some trees. (168)

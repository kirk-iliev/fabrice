## Bolazec (France) — January 16, 1966 (0400)

On the Morlaix-Callac Road, 23- year-old Eugene Coquil was driving his Renault Dauphine when he saw headlights in a field and observed an object come over his car and land on the other side of the road. It was square with four vertical lights resembling lamps. The witness drove away and did not see the object leave. {Personal; 161)

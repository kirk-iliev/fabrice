## Birac (France) — October 11, 1954 (2150)

Four persons, among them Julia Juste, Maria Barbeau, and Marion Le Tanneur, of Jarnac, saw two luminous spheres flying in the same direction. Having stopped and maneuvered in mid- air, the larger one took on a deep red color, turned to an intense white light with a red aura, and both landed, Witnesses did not wait. (Quincy; M 160)

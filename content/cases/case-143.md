## Mourieras (France) — September 10, 1954 (2030)

A farmer, Mr. Mazaud, was walking home when he was suddenly confronted with a helmeted being of average height who made friendly gestures, then went back into the brush, entered a cigar-shaped object about 4 m long, which took off toward Limoges. A few minutes later, witnesses in Limoges reported a disk-shaped, red object leaving a bluish trail. (7;M40)
